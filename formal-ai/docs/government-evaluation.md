# Russian verb government evaluation

Government implementation in `src/sources/government.mjs`, integrated through `src/resolve.mjs`. Offline tests are in `tests/formal-ai/government.test.mjs`; the strict request fixture in `tests/formal-ai/resolve.test.mjs` was adapted to the concurrent capital-title batching change.

The parser reads meaning-specific `+obj` annotations, explicit case qualifiers, Russian management sections and case-only usage qualifiers, and highlighted examples with recipient pronouns. Explicit cross-language subject mapping enables experiencer reversal. Aspect partners keep the selected frame. No vocabulary overrides or per-word translation rules were added.

## Remaining problems

- “Message” still falls through translation pointers to SMS-specific verbs; the available translation tables do not supply the requested broader Russian verb.
- The public-data-only “answer” entry has no usable case annotation for the question object in the fetched entries, so its accusative fallback still omits `на`.
- The concurrent phrase/parser work currently leaves the Tell batch line untranslated and parses the Send recipient incorrectly, equally in the controlled before/after runs. These lines were rendered correctly at the beginning of this task.
- Noun/sense selection remains imperfect (for example, “stores” can select warehouses). Several website fragments are already incomplete in the baseline.
- Russian case-question notes alone cannot prove experiencer reversal: an explicit cross-language subject mapping is required, so ordinary dative-object verbs retain their subjects.
