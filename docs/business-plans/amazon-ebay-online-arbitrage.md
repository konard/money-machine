## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$300 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $8,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **6/10**. Expect the first payment after 21–90 d.

## How it works

Using tools like Keepa and SellerAmp you find products whose Amazon/eBay sell price exceeds retail-clearance or online-deal cost by enough to cover fees (Amazon US referral 15% in most categories, 8% electronics, FBA fees, $39.99/mo Professional plan or $0.99/item Individual; eBay ~13.6% + $0.30-0.40/order). You buy units, ship them to an Amazon warehouse (FBA) or list on eBay, and Amazon handles fulfillment. Amazon pays out every ~14 days. Margins are thin (~15-30% ROI).

**Platforms:** Amazon Seller Central (FBA), eBay, Keepa, SellerAmp, Tactical Arbitrage

### Pricing strategy

Buy only at 30%+ ROI after all fees; price at or just below the Buy Box and reprice automatically, raising when competitors stock out.

## Legality & compliance

Reselling genuine goods is legal (first-sale/exhaustion doctrine); some brands gated, counterfeits prohibited.

- **Compliance requirements:** Amazon seller identity verification; sales tax/VAT registration where required; keep supplier invoices; product safety rules (EU GPSR)
- **Platform availability:** Amazon lists ~27 store countries and accepts sellers from many more (needs an internationally chargeable card and a bank account; Payoneer for unsupported banks); exact registration-country list not confirmed. Works best for residents of US/UK/EU/CA/JP/AU. PHYSICAL STEP: buying and shipping inventory.
