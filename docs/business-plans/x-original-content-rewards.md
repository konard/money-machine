## How it works

X retired Creator Revenue Sharing (new enrolments stopped 7 Aug 2026; last payout ~11 Sep 2026) and launched Original Content Rewards. You need an X Premium subscription and to meet thresholds (500+ verified followers, 500K+ Home Timeline impressions from verified users in the last 90 days excluding replies, 18+, eligible country, account in good standing). Pay is based on qualified impressions on original posts; cross-platform reposts and copied content are excluded, commentary must add value.
