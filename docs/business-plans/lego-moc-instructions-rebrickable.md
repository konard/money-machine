## How it works

You build a model digitally in BrickLink Studio (free), generate step-by-step instructions, and list it as a Premium MOC on Rebrickable. Buyers pay for the instructions and use Rebrickable to buy parts or match their own collection. Payment goes straight to your PayPal or Stripe account; Rebrickable then deducts or invoices its commission (reported 8%/13%/18% by price band on its Designer Plan). Popular MOC designers also get approached by kit sellers for licensing.
