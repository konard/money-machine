## How it works

You write original stories and send them through free submission portals to professional genre and literary magazines. Asimov's pays 8-10 cents a word for stories up to 7,500 words and $1 a line for poetry, with replies in about five weeks. Uncanny pays $0.10 a word and $40 a poem but was closed to all submissions in Nov 2026. Rejections are normal, so you keep a submission tracker and resend rejected stories. Pro markets ban AI-written or AI-assisted work, so AI is limited to market research and tracking. Fee-charging contests are excluded.
