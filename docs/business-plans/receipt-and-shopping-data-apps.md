## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$2–$8 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $40 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **9/10**. Expect the first payment after 14–90 d.

## How it works

Market-research apps buy anonymised purchase data by rewarding receipt uploads. Receipt Hog pays points for any receipt and cashes out to PayPal; Ibotta pays cash rebates on specific products to PayPal or bank; Shopmium (France, UK, Belgium, US) pays rebates to PayPal or bank. Nothing changes in how you shop; a weekly photo session yields a few dollars a month. Avoid gift-card-only apps (Fetch) if you want money in a bank.
