## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$500 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $9,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **4/10**. Expect the first payment after 14–120 d.

## How it works

L&D teams and training firms pay per course or per finished learning-minute. You restructure source material with an LLM into objectives, modules and assessments, build in an authoring tool or open-source exporters, add AI narration with commercial rights, and package as SCORM 1.2/xAPI for the client's LMS. Compliance and onboarding training recur yearly.

**Platforms:** Upwork, direct to L&D teams and training vendors

## What can be automated

- LLM course outlines and quiz generation from SOPs
- AI narration and captions
- scripted SCORM packaging and LMS test uploads (e.g. SCORM Cloud)

## Unavoidable manual steps

- SME review sessions
- instructional-design decisions
- LMS-specific troubleshooting

## Legality & compliance

Legal; taxable. Content accuracy remains the SME's responsibility; regulated training (safety, medical) needs client sign-off.

- **Compliance requirements:** tax reporting; client IP/NDA; licensed AI voices and media; accessibility (captions, WCAG) where required
- **Platform availability:** Direct invoicing (Wise/Payoneer) and Upwork pay out in nearly all reference countries.

## Risks

- long B2B sales cycles
- authoring-tool license costs (free/open-source options exist)
- LMS compatibility issues
