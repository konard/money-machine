## How it works

Tabletop gamers with resin printers subscribe ($5-15/month) for a monthly release of pre-supported miniatures and terrain. Thangs Memberships charges a 14% platform fee (+Stripe fees); Printables Clubs launched with a 10% fee and Stripe payments (tiers $3-100); MyMiniFactory Tribes earnings are withdrawn via PayPal on the 14th/28th. Unlike generic Patreon, files are delivered automatically to a 3D-print search audience. Back-catalogue sets are later sold as bundles.

## Payout path

- **Methods:** Thangs/Printables: Stripe -> bank, MyMiniFactory: payout request -> PayPal (14th and 28th for Pro/Studio; 28th only for Upcoming) or bank transfer (monthly, 28th)
- **Minimum payout:** No minimum stated by Thangs, Printables or MyMiniFactory; MyMiniFactory charges a $2 processing fee on payouts under $100
- **Frequency:** Thangs monthly; MyMiniFactory twice-monthly on request; Printables via Stripe
- **Typical delay:** 30 days
- **Notes:** MyMiniFactory Tribes requires the Pro ($24.99/mo) or Studio ($99.99/mo) creator plan (store commission 15/12.5/10% by tier + 3.49% + $0.49 processing); the Tribes-specific commission is not published on the creator help center. Thangs membership fee 14% and Printables Clubs 10% confirmed on official pages.

## What can be automated

- Sculpting each month's set (ZBrush/Blender) - heavy manual art work
- Test resin prints
- Community management

## Legality & compliance

Legal everywhere; sculpts must be original (no Games Workshop/Disney likenesses).

- **Compliance requirements:** report income for tax; Stripe/PayPal KYC; original IP only
- **Platform availability:** MyMiniFactory pays by PayPal (45/50 reference countries) or bank; Thangs memberships limited to its Stripe country list (~21/50 reference countries); Printables Clubs via Stripe (limited country list).

## Risks

- Monthly content treadmill; churn when releases slip
- Piracy of STL packs
- Paid MMF tier is a fixed cost
- Saturated fantasy-mini niche
