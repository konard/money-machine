## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$5 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $20,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **3/10**. Expect the first payment after 45–365 d.

## How it works

Join the Fortnite Developer Program (18+), build an island in UEFN and publish it. Epic puts 40% of eligible net Item Shop revenue into an Engagement Pool and splits it monthly by playtime, retention and new-player acquisition, counting only paying players. Islands can also sell items for V-Bucks: the creator gets 50% of the V-Bucks value, or 100% from 9 Jan 2026 to 31 Jan 2027. Discovery is winner-take-most.

**Platforms:** Fortnite Creative / UEFN, Epic Creator Portal
