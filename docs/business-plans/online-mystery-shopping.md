## How it works

Brands hire mystery-shopping firms to audit their service. Some assignments can be done entirely online: placing a web inquiry, testing live chat, calling a call centre or completing an online ordering flow. You then fill in a detailed questionnaire. Pay is typically $5-25 per online or phone shop, and many shops reimburse purchases. Most shops still need in-person visits, so online-only volume is small. Never pay to join, and treat any 'deposit this cheque' request as a scam.

**Platforms:** MSPA-member firms (e.g. Market Force, BestMark, Intellishop)
