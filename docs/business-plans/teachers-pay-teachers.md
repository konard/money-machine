## How it works

TPT is the largest marketplace for teacher-made resources. Sellers upload PDFs or digital activities and set prices. Basic Sellers pay a one-time $29 fee and keep 55% of each sale minus $0.30 per resource; Exclusive Sellers pay $59.95 a year, must sell their digital resources only on TPT, and keep 80% (77.5% from 1 January 2027) minus $0.15 per resource on orders under $3. Premium Seller memberships closed to new sign-ups on 1 October 2026. Sales cluster around back-to-school and holidays. AI can help draft and format materials, but buyers punish low-quality uploads with reviews.

### Pricing strategy

Price single resources at $3-5 and offer a free sample to collect reviews, then sell bundles at a 20-30% discount and raise prices on best-sellers.
