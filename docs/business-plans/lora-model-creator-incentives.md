## How it works

Model-hosting platforms pay creators when their models are used in online generation. Tensor.Art restructured its creator incentives in July 2026 (TenStar Fund, Pro Model and Pro Tools incentives) with daily earnings updates. You train LoRAs on data you own or that is licensed (your own art, CC0, your photos), publish them with clear license terms, and keep them SFW. Agents can curate datasets, caption, train and publish variants.

## Payout path

- **Methods:** platform wallet -> bank transfer
- **Minimum payout:** $50 wallet balance (TensorHub)
- **Frequency:** earnings credited daily (TensorArt) / weekly Monday (TensorHub); withdrawals processed weekly, usually Mondays
- **Typical delay:** 10 days
- **Notes:** TensorHub pays all original public models automatically (no application). Since 22 Jul 2026 the main TensorArt site only pays incentives on Pro-user usage of approved Pro models/tools or designated new base models, and creators report sharply lower rates. Per-run rates are not published.
