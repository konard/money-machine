## How it works

Small sites pay $50-$500 for a clear audit. You crawl the site (crawler, PageSpeed API, client-granted Search Console), feed findings to an LLM report template, review and prioritize. Upsell fixes or monitoring.

**Platforms:** Fiverr, Upwork, Contra, own site
