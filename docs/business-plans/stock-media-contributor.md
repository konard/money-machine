## How it works

You upload keyworded media; agencies sell licenses to marketers and designers and pay you a royalty (Adobe Stock ~33% images/35% video). Adobe Stock accepts generative-AI content if labeled 'Created using generative AI tools', with releases for any real people and tool licenses allowing commercial use. Shutterstock and Pond5 do NOT accept AI-generated submissions, so only your own photos/footage go there. Volume of high-demand, well-keyworded assets drives income.

**Platforms:** Adobe Stock, Shutterstock, Pond5
