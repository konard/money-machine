## How it works

You build a template (landing page, portfolio, SaaS site) and publish it on Framer Marketplace (creator keeps 100% of paid template revenue plus commissions when people upgrade via your remix link), Webflow Marketplace (95% to creator) or the Shopify Theme Store (strict review; 15% revenue share on gross theme sales). Buyers are founders, freelancers and merchants. Free templates can still earn via referral commissions.

**Platforms:** Framer Marketplace, Webflow Marketplace, Shopify Theme Store
