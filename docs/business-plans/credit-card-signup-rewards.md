## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$80 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $400 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **7/10**. Expect the first payment after 60–150 d.

## How it works

Offers are country-specific. Example checked Nov 2026: the US Capital One Venture card gives 75,000 miles (about $750 in travel) after $4,000 of purchases in the first 3 months, with a $95 annual fee. UK, Canadian and Australian issuers offer smaller points packages. You route only existing spending through the card and pay the full statement each month, so there is no interest. Points/cashback are redeemed as statement credit, bank transfer or travel. Issuers pay because interchange fees and future revolving balances from the average customer fund it.

**Platforms:** US issuers (Chase, Amex, Capital One, Citi), UK Amex/Barclaycard, Canadian and Australian card issuers

## Legality & compliance

Legal everywhere; in the US cash rewards from spending are generally treated as rebates (not income), bank-account-style bonuses may be taxable.

- **Compliance requirements:** good credit history; never carry a balance; honest income declaration on applications; follow issuer 'once per lifetime' rules
- **Platform availability:** Country-specific: lucrative bonuses are concentrated in the US, Canada, UK and Australia; elsewhere card rewards are small. Requires a credit file, so new arrivals and thin-file applicants are usually declined. Offers change frequently; check issuer pages.

## Risks

- Interest and debt if balance not paid
- Credit score dips from hard pulls
- Annual fees
- Account shutdowns for gaming (manufactured spend)
