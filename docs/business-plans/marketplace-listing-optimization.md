## How it works

Sellers pay because listing copy and images drive conversion. You audit a listing, research keywords from marketplace autocomplete and the seller's own data, rewrite copy with an LLM within each marketplace's style rules, and design A+ modules and infographics. Brand owners add you as a Brand Registry user rather than sharing logins. Sell per-ASIN packages and monthly catalog retainers.

**Platforms:** Upwork, Fiverr, direct (Amazon/Etsy sellers)
