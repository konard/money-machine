## How it works

You build Google Sheets/Excel templates with formulas, dashboards and instructions (budgets, small-business bookkeeping, wedding planners, KPI trackers) and sell them on Etsy or Gumroad. Buyers pay for a ready-to-use tool rather than building it. Google Sheets copy links make delivery trivial. Avoid presenting outputs as personalized financial advice.

**Platforms:** Etsy, Gumroad, Payhip
