## How it works

AI video model labs buy licensed footage; brokers (Troveo, Calliope Networks) aggregate creator archives and pay roughly $1-$4 per minute depending on quality and format. You must own the footage and have releases for identifiable people. An agent can inventory and tag archives, strip unwanted segments, prepare metadata and handle uploads. One-off or recurring payments as new footage is added.

**Platforms:** Troveo, Calliope Networks, other licensed AI data brokers

## Payout path

- **Methods:** Tipalti -> bank transfer (Troveo), broker-dependent for others
- **Minimum payout:** none published (Troveo)
- **Frequency:** per licensing deal; no fixed schedule
- **Typical delay:** 60 days
- **Notes:** Troveo pays only after your content is licensed, the AI client is invoiced and pays; non-US licensors submit W-8BEN. Troveo does not publish per-minute rates; the ~$1-$4/minute range comes from press reports.

## Legality & compliance

Legal if you own copyright and have releases; check that footage doesn't include others' likenesses or copyrighted music.

- **Compliance requirements:** report income for tax; copyright ownership; model/property releases; privacy law for people in footage
- **Platform availability:** Troveo pays international licensors via Tipalti bank transfer; exact country list not published.

## Risks

- Requires an existing archive
