## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$80 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $4,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **3/10**. Expect the first payment after 21–180 d.

## How it works

You upload designs to Printful or Printify, which sync products to your Etsy or Shopify store. When a customer buys, the POD provider charges your card the base cost and prints/ships directly (PHYSICAL STEP handled by the provider, not you). You keep retail minus base cost and marketplace fees; a $28 tee nets roughly $11 on Etsy with a standard provider and no ads (less with premium providers or ad spend).

**Platforms:** Printful, Printify, Etsy, Shopify

## Payout path

- **Methods:** Etsy Payments / Shopify Payments -> bank, PayPal (Shopify)
- **Minimum payout:** Per sales-channel rules (Etsy: none on weekly schedule; Shopify Payments: none)
- **Frequency:** daily to weekly
- **Typical delay:** 5 days
- **Notes:** Printful/Printify charge you the base cost + shipping per order (no monthly fee); profit arrives via the sales channel. Etsy new-seller funds are held ~14 days.

## Legality & compliance

Legal; PHYSICAL fulfilment and shipping is outsourced to the POD provider. Consumer-protection/returns rules apply.

- **Compliance requirements:** report income for tax; no trademark/copyright infringement; consumer return obligations; sales tax/VAT handled per channel
- **Platform availability:** Printful/Printify connect to Etsy and Shopify globally. Etsy Payments covers ~26 of ~50 reference countries; Shopify Payments only 41 countries (mostly EU/OECD), though Shopify stores elsewhere can use PayPal or local gateways. Shopify also costs a monthly subscription after the trial.

## Risks

- IP takedowns and account suspensions
- Shipping delays cause refunds
