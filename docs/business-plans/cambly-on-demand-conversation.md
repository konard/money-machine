## How it works

Cambly connects English learners to tutors for on-demand or scheduled conversation calls. You are paid a fixed $0.17/minute ($10.20/hour), or $0.20/minute for Cambly Kids, only for minutes actually spent in calls. Students pay Cambly a subscription; you do not set prices or market yourself. Fluency in English is the main requirement; native-speaker preference varies.

**Platforms:** Cambly, Cambly Kids
