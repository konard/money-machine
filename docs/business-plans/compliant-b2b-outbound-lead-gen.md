## How it works

B2B firms pay for booked meetings. You define the ideal customer, source business contacts from licensed data vendors, personalize emails with an LLM, and send low-volume campaigns from client domains with unsubscribe link and postal address. Replies are triaged and meetings booked. Honor opt-outs (US: 10 business days) and consent rules of each recipient country.

**Platforms:** Upwork, direct, licensed contact-data vendors

## Legality & compliance

Restrictions are on recipients located in these countries, not on where the operator lives. US: CAN-SPAM has no B2B exception but is opt-out (honor within 10 business days). UK: ICO confirms PECR email consent rule does not cover corporate subscribers (companies, LLPs, Scottish partnerships); sole traders and some partnerships need consent or soft opt-in. Germany and Canada verified from statute; Netherlands from secondary sources (statute page not parsed). Other likely opt-in recipient markets (Italy, South Korea, Japan partially) not verified, so coverage kept at 88 (~44/50) rather than 94. Never email consumers or use scraped lists.

- **Restricted or banned in:** Germany (recipients: UWG s.7(2) no.2 requires prior express consent for email ads to any addressee, incl. businesses), Netherlands (recipients: Telecommunicatiewet art. 11.7(1) consent also applies to legal persons), Canada (recipients: CASL consent; implied only for conspicuously published addresses relevant to the recipient's role)
- **Compliance requirements:** CAN-SPAM: honest headers, postal address, opt-out in 10 business days; GDPR/PECR legitimate-interest assessment; CASL where applicable; licensed data only; no LinkedIn automation bots
- **Platform availability:** Direct invoicing works almost everywhere; legality depends on recipient country, not operator country.
