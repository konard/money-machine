## How it works

SMBs selling to larger firms face long security questionnaires and need written policies. An LLM drafts answers from the client's verified control inventory. You also run phishing-awareness simulations with Gophish (free, open-source) or a commercial platform, only under signed authorization for the client's own staff and domains. Packages renew yearly.

**Platforms:** Upwork, direct, Gophish (free open-source phishing-simulation framework), commercial phishing-simulation platforms (partner programs for MSPs/consultants, e.g. KnowBe4)

## Legality & compliance

Legal with written authorization; unauthorized phishing/testing is a crime nearly everywhere. Answers must be truthful and attested by the client.

- **Compliance requirements:** signed scope/authorization letter for simulations; client employees only; no credential harvesting kept; truthful questionnaire answers signed by client; data-protection notice to employees where required (EU/UK works councils); tax reporting
- **Platform availability:** Direct invoicing (Wise/Payoneer) and Upwork pay out in nearly all reference countries.
