## How it works

List fixed-scope gigs: PDF/scan to Excel, CSV cleanup, CRM dedupe, sheet merges. Small businesses pay because the work is tedious. A pipeline (OCR, pandas, LLM extraction) does most of each order; you spot-check and deliver. Repeat buyers send monthly batches.

**Platforms:** Fiverr, Upwork, Contra

### Pricing strategy

Start with a $10-$20 basic tier capped by row/page count to collect the first 10 reviews, then reprice per 1,000 rows/pages and push monthly batch packages at $100+.

### Path to profit

1. Month 0: build an OCR + pandas + LLM extraction pipeline and 3 gigs with sample before/after files (cost $0-$20)
2. Month 1-2: first 5-15 small orders at $10-$30, expected $50-$200 gross
3. Month 3-6: raise prices after reviews, add bulk/recurring tiers, bid on Upwork batch jobs
4. Month 6-12: convert repeat buyers to monthly retainers; target 10-20 orders/month

## Legality & compliance

- **Compliance requirements:** tax reporting; platform ID check; Fiverr AI standards / Upwork no-misrepresentation; data-processing care for personal data
