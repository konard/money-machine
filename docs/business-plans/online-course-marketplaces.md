## How it works

You produce a structured video course and publish on Udemy (37% of organic marketplace sales, 97% via your own coupon/referral link; subscription revenue share dropped to 15% on Jan 1, 2026) and/or Skillshare (monthly royalty pool split by minutes watched; since July 2026 you need 50 followers plus 400+ qualifying minutes/month if you published a class in the last 18 months, otherwise 650+). Self-hosting keeps more per sale but needs your own audience: Thinkific has a free plan, Payhip's free plan charges 5%, Teachable has no free plan (from $39/mo + 7.5%).

- **Frequency:** monthly
- **Typical delay:** 40 days
- **Notes:** Udemy pays roughly 30+ days after month end (after refund window); Skillshare pays on the 16th for the previous month's minutes.
