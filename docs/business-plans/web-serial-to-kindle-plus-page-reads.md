## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$150 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $10,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **3/10**. Expect the first payment after 60–365 d.

## How it works

You post chapters of a long genre serial (LitRPG, progression fantasy) on Royal Road on a strict schedule to build followers. Readers who want to read ahead pay a Patreon tier for advance chapters. Each finished arc becomes a book enrolled in KDP Select, where Amazon pays from a monthly Global Fund per first-time KENP page read (a mechanic distinct from per-copy sales). Exclusivity means the free web version is cut back before enrolment.

**Platforms:** Royal Road, Patreon, Amazon KDP Select (Kindle+)
