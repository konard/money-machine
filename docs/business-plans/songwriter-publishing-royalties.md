## How it works

Streaming distributors pay only master royalties; the composition side (performance and mechanical) is collected by PROs (ASCAP/BMI/PRS/SOCAN etc.), The MLC for US streaming mechanicals, or an admin like Songtrust worldwide. You register songs, splits and ISWCs, and report live and livestreamed setlists through PRO online performance-reporting programs. Money that would otherwise go unclaimed flows to you.

**Platforms:** The MLC, Songtrust, ASCAP / BMI / PRS for Music / local PRO

## Payout path

- **Methods:** bank transfer (direct deposit), PayPal (some admins)
- **Minimum payout:** PRS: £30 (UK) / £60 (international); The MLC pays monthly; others vary by society
- **Frequency:** monthly (MLC) to quarterly (PROs)
- **Typical delay:** 120 days
- **Notes:** Verified: The MLC is free and distributes monthly; Songtrust charges a one-time $100 fee plus 15% of performance / 20% of mechanical royalties. ASCAP is free to join as a writer, and ASCAP OnStage pays royalties for live sets at ASCAP-licensed venues, with claims due one quarter after the performance quarter (classical/recital and non-licensable events are excluded); payment by direct deposit. PRS for Music charges a one-off £100 joining fee (£30 under 25; MCPS is a separate £100), pays once earnings exceed £30 UK / £60 international, and lets members register live performances in the dashboard. BMI fees and BMI Live rules could not be fetched (bmi.com returned 403); livestream eligibility under OnStage is not stated.

## Legality & compliance

Collective rights management is legal in all reference countries; most have a national CMO, though local societies vary in efficiency.

- **Compliance requirements:** only one PRO per territory; accurate splits and co-writer agreements; tax forms for foreign societies; PRS has a £100 joining fee; ASCAP and The MLC are free
- **Platform availability:** Every reference country has access to at least one PRO (local or foreign); The MLC accepts anyone owed US mechanicals. Payout is direct deposit to bank.
