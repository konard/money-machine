## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$150 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $10,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **3/10**. Expect the first payment after 30–365 d.

## How it works

Only approved Partners can publish Bedrock skin packs, maps, add-ons and textures to the Marketplace, and Mojang wants a portfolio and a business-like operation. Mojang suggests starting by working with an existing partner, so for an individual the realistic path is paid contract work for a partner studio, then applying directly. Partners earn a share of Minecoin sales.

**Platforms:** Minecraft Marketplace (Bedrock), Minecraft Partner Program, partner studios (e.g. Noxcrew, CrackedCubes)

## What can be automated

- Generate skin variations and texture drafts with image models, then hand-finish them
- Use AI to write Bedrock add-on JSON/scripts
- Auto-render marketing screenshots with scripted camera paths

## Unavoidable manual steps

- Build a public portfolio (free maps/packs on CurseForge/MCPEDL)
- Pitch partner studios or apply to the Partner Program
- Quality and art direction to pass Mojang review

## Legality & compliance

Legal creative work. Content must be original and all-ages, and must follow Minecraft Usage Guidelines.

- **Compliance requirements:** Partner application and agreement (business entity often expected); Original content only; Tax reporting as contractor/business
- **Platform availability:** Partner acceptance is selective, and the countries partners can be in are not published. Contract work for studios is available wherever the studio can pay by PayPal/Wise. Estimate unverified.

## Risks

- Gatekept: most applicants are not accepted
- Crowded catalogue and falling per-pack sales
- Revenue terms are opaque and under NDA
