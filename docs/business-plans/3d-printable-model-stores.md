## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$30 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $2,500 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **4/10**. Expect the first payment after 14–180 d.

## How it works

You model and test-print functional or decorative objects (organisers, mounts, cosplay parts, replacement parts) and list STL/3MF files; hobbyists buy and print at home. Cults3D reportedly pays designers 80% of the sale; Printables Store charges a 20% fee (min. price $5, Stripe payouts); Thangs takes 30%/20%/15% for items under $5/$5-10/$10+ plus Stripe fees. Alternative: Bambu Lab's MakerWorld is free-download only, but models enrolled in its Exclusive Model Program earn Exclusive Points worth $0.066 each that can be cashed out per $100 earned (eligibility after 100 total prints on your account). Free uploads build followers who buy paid models; parametric OpenSCAD designs yield many variants.

**Platforms:** Cults3D, Printables Store (Prusa), Thangs, MakerWorld Exclusive Model Program (alternative)

## Legality & compliance

Selling original 3D designs is legal everywhere; do not sell files of trademarked characters or replicas of copyrighted products; weapons files are prohibited by platforms and illegal in some countries.

- **Compliance requirements:** report income for tax; Stripe/PayPal KYC; no IP-infringing or weapon models
- **Platform availability:** Thangs payouts limited to its Stripe country list (~21/50 reference countries incl. US, UK, Canada, EU members, Japan, Korea, Taiwan, India, Indonesia, Malaysia, Philippines, Mexico, Argentina, Morocco, Singapore). Printables pays only via Stripe (limited list). Cults3D reportedly pays via PayPal (45/50 reference countries, unverified). MakerWorld cash-outs go to a bank account via its payout provider (country coverage unverified).
