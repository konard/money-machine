## How it works

You design a card or board game, upload art files, set a retail price above TGC's manufacturing cost, and list it in their shop. TGC manufactures and ships each copy (physical step done by the platform, not you). Royalty = 70% of (price - cost); e.g. $15 price - $8 cost = $7 profit, you get $4.90. Payouts run every Monday after a 30-day hold. Contests on TGC and print-and-play versions help gain traction.
