## How it works

You publish a niche show (interviews, news recaps, explainers) on Spotify for Creators. The Spotify Partner Program pays ad revenue and Premium-engagement payouts once you have 3+ episodes, 1,000 unique listeners and 2,000 consumption hours in 30 days (lowered Jan 2026). Spotify added a host-read sponsorship tool (2026) and fan Memberships (Oct 2026); direct sponsor deals also pay. Podcorn, the former sponsorship marketplace, now redirects to a "sunset" notice. AI can handle research, editing, show notes and clips; synthetic hosts must be disclosed.

**Platforms:** Spotify for Creators, Direct sponsors
