## How it works

Companies recruit interviewees through platforms like Respondent and User Interviews. You fill a profile, apply to studies that match, and if selected join a video call or complete a diary study. Pay is set per study (Respondent: most sessions $50–$250 depending on length and seniority, senior B2B roles $400+). Payment is sent through Tremendous (PayPal, bank or gift card) after the session is verified. Selection is competitive, so apply to many studies; professionals with specific job titles are picked far more often.
