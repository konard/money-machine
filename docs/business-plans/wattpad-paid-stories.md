## How it works

You publish serialized original fiction free on Wattpad and build reads, votes and comments. In October 2023 Wattpad replaced Paid Stories with Wattpad Originals, a freemium model: some chapters stay free, others are unlocked with Coins or a Premium+ subscription. Originals is invite-only by editorial selection, and from September 2024 Wattpad also took pitches, with accepted pitches eligible for up to $5,000 in advances plus an ongoing revenue share. Wattpad has not published the revenue-share percentage. Wattpad Studios/Webtoon Productions may also option rights.
