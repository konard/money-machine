## How it works

EarnKaro is an Indian sub-affiliate network: it holds the merchant affiliate relationships and pays you a share when people buy through your links. You run a deal channel or content page and post converted links. Profit shows as pending within 72 hours and is confirmed after the retailer's return window. You avoid applying to each Indian brand's program separately.
