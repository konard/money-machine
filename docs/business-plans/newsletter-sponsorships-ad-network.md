## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$150 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $10,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **4/10**. Expect the first payment after 60–270 d.

## How it works

Run a focused newsletter (e.g. weekly AI tools for accountants) and earn when advertisers pay for placements: CPC/CPM via the beehiiv Ad Network, or flat-fee sponsorships booked through marketplaces (Paved, Passionfroot) or directly. Payers are brands buying attention of your subscribers. Revenue scales with engaged, real subscribers; buying lists or bot subscribers is prohibited and destroys deliverability.

**Platforms:** beehiiv Ad Network, Paved, Passionfroot, direct sponsors

## Payout path

- **Methods:** Stripe Express -> bank, bank transfer, PayPal
- **Minimum payout:** beehiiv: no stated minimum (Stripe Express via beehiiv Wallet); Paved: $1,000 balance threshold (lower balances roll over)
- **Frequency:** monthly (beehiiv on the 20th; Paved monthly) / per campaign for direct sponsors
- **Typical delay:** 50 days
- **Notes:** beehiiv pays Ad Network earnings on the 20th for the previous month's placements (reports ~96h after send); PayPal/Wise not supported. Paved's publisher terms: paid within 60 days after month end once the $1,000 threshold is met; the marketplace platform fee is shown in-platform, not a fixed published %.

## Legality & compliance

Legal; sponsored content must be labelled; email laws (CAN-SPAM, GDPR/PECR) apply to list building.

- **Compliance requirements:** label sponsored content; affiliate disclosure (FTC/CAP); double opt-in / GDPR consent; CAN-SPAM unsubscribe
- **Platform availability:** beehiiv Ad Network is open to anyone on a paid beehiiv plan who is actively sending (no subscriber minimum, but small lists get fewer offers) and requires Stripe Express in your country. Paved/Passionfroot favour English-language audiences. Direct sponsorships need no platform.
