## How it works

Online RPG groups pay for tokens, battle maps, character sheets and automated modules. Roll20 Marketplace sells creator art packs and adventures with a revenue share. Foundry VTT opened its own official Marketplace on 12 Feb 2025; creators can also sell premium modules via their own store (activation keys) or Patreon-linked access. Battle maps can be produced in Dungeondraft/Wonderdraft with consistent asset packs.

**Platforms:** Roll20 Marketplace, Foundry VTT Marketplace / premium packages
