## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$500 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $8,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **5/10**. Expect the first payment after 14–90 d.

## How it works

Many creators can reach 2-5x more viewers with dubbed audio tracks (YouTube supports multi-language audio). You run their video through AI transcription, translation, voice cloning (with their consent) and lip-sync tools, then a native-speaker check where needed, and deliver files or upload to their channel. Clients pay per minute or a monthly retainer. An agent handles the whole pipeline; humans sell and QA.

**Platforms:** ElevenLabs Dubbing / similar tools, Stripe/Wise invoicing, direct clients
