## How it works

Publish neutral, factual guides (e.g. 'how freelancers in Kenya receive USD', fee comparisons) and include your official referral/partner link for apps like Wise, Revolut or Interactive Brokers. Programs pay fixed rewards per qualifying new customer: Wise's reward is set per region and shown on your invite page; IBKR pays $200 per referral (max 15/year, 30 total) but requires the referrer's own account to hold at least $2,000 and have made a securities trade. Never give personalised investment advice.

## Legality & compliance

Referral rewards are legal; financial-promotion rules (e.g. UK FCA) apply to investment content: stay factual, add risk warnings, avoid crypto/leveraged products.

- **Restricted or banned in:** IBKR referral: residents of Japan, Denmark, Spain, Portugal, Poland, mainland China and Israel excluded (Japan is the only one in the reference set), countries where a given app is not licensed
- **Compliance requirements:** financial promotion rules: no personalised advice; program terms; affiliate disclosure (FTC/CAP); tax on rewards
- **Platform availability:** Each app licenses only certain countries; Wise and Payoneer cover most of the reference set, Revolut mainly EEA/UK/US/AU/SG/JP/BR/MX; IBKR excludes some countries.
