## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$30 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $8,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **3/10**. Expect the first payment after 60–365 d.

## How it works

You build a site on a cheap domain/host, generating templated-but-valuable pages from real datasets (programmatic SEO) and editorial articles with AI assistance. Visitors from Google/Bing see ads; AdSense pays from day one of approval, and at scale you move to premium networks (Ezoic: no pageview minimum; Journey by Mediavine: 1,000 Tier-1 sessions in 30 days; full Mediavine: $5,000+ annual ad revenue; Raptive: 25k monthly pageviews with 50%+ tier-1 traffic below 100k). Google's scaled-content-abuse policy penalizes mass low-value AI pages, so each page needs unique data or utility.

**Platforms:** Google AdSense, Ezoic, Mediavine Journey, Raptive
