## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$10 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $500 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **2/10**. Expect the first payment after 30–365 d.

## How it works

Poe lets eligible creators set a price per message on their bots and also pays referral bonuses for subscriptions; you build a bot on top of an LLM (or your own server bot) with a valuable prompt/workflow. OpenAI's GPT Store builder revenue program is still a small US-only pilot not accepting new builders, so it should not be relied on. Payers are platform users (via the platform). Expect tiny earnings unless a bot goes viral.
