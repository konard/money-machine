## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$40 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $3,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **3/10**. Expect the first payment after 30–365 d.

## How it works

Publish a Patreon page with 2-4 tiers and post exclusive content (files, tutorials, early access) on a schedule. Patrons are existing followers of your free work who pay to support you. Without an audience conversion is near zero; typically 1-5% of engaged followers convert.

Patreon rewards creators who already have an engaged audience (YouTube, podcasts, webcomics); typical conversion is ~1-5% of active fans. New creators without traffic rarely get past a handful of patrons. The 2025 move to a single 10% plan raised fees for new pages, and Apple in-app pricing changes added friction.

### Pricing strategy

Launch with a low $3-$5 tier plus one higher tier with tangible perks; raise prices for new patrons and add annual billing once churn stabilises.

### Path to profit

1. Month 0: set up page and 2-3 tiers (cost $0)
2. Month 1-2: announce to existing audience; first 5-20 patrons
3. Month 3-6: regular exclusive posts, reduce churn
4. Month 6-12: $30-$300/month for creators with 5k+ engaged followers
