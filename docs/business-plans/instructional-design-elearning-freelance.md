## Realistic expectations

## How it works

Companies, NGOs and course creators need their knowledge turned into structured training: learning objectives, storyboards, interactive modules (Articulate Rise/Storyline, iSpring) and assessments exported as SCORM for their LMS. You take fixed-price projects on Upwork or win direct clients. LLMs draft outlines, scripts, quiz banks and voiceovers, and you design, QA and package. The payer is a business, not a learner, so budgets are larger than tutoring rates.

**Platforms:** Upwork, Contra, direct clients

### Pricing strategy

Win first clients with fixed-price small modules ($200-500) using free tools, then quote per finished minute or per project at $40-70/hr equivalent once you have 3-5 portfolio pieces.
