## How it works

On a paid beehiiv plan (Lite from $49/mo billed annually, $59 monthly; Ad Network 'not in trial; not in all regions') you are offered sponsor campaigns each billing cycle; you place the ad and get paid per unique open (CPM) or unique click (CPC), as set by each advertiser. Boosts pay you a fee per subscriber you recommend to other newsletters. Payers are advertisers and other publishers, distinct from reader subscriptions. Content can be AI-assisted curation, but advertisers drop low-engagement lists.
