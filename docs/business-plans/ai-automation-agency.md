## Legality & compliance

Software consulting is legal everywhere. Client data handling triggers GDPR-style obligations; cold outreach must follow anti-spam laws (CAN-SPAM, GDPR/PECR, CASL).

- **Compliance requirements:** data processing agreement where needed; anti-spam compliance for outreach; tax reporting; n8n Sustainable Use License: build workflows on the client's own n8n instance or buy a commercial licence; do not resell hosted n8n access
- **Platform availability:** Upwork 180+ countries; Contra pays out via local bank (Airwallex/Stripe), PayPal, Payoneer or crypto; Zapier partners must not be in US-sanctioned countries and need a business website and custom-domain email. Direct invoicing via Wise/Payoneer works almost everywhere.
