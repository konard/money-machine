## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$20–$150 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $3,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **9/10**. Expect the first payment after 3–21 d.

## How it works

Start by decluttering your own items for immediate cash, then optionally source underpriced goods from thrift stores, estate sales or free listings. AI can write titles/descriptions and price from sold comps. Buyers pay through the platform; you ship (or meet locally) and the platform releases funds after delivery. Vinted charges the buyer a protection fee so sellers keep the item price; eBay US charges 13.6% + $0.30-0.40 per order in most categories (250 free listings/month); Mercari US fees have changed repeatedly, so check its current fee page.

**Platforms:** eBay, Vinted, Mercari, Poshmark, Depop, Facebook Marketplace, OLX, Jiji, Carousell

## Legality & compliance

Selling your own used goods is legal everywhere. Regular resale is a business; tax above thresholds (DAC7, 1099-K).

- **Compliance requirements:** declare income above thresholds (DAC7/1099-K); no counterfeit or prohibited items; honest item condition
- **Platform availability:** eBay operates in ~190 markets; regional platforms (Vinted EU/UK, Mercari US/JP, Carousell SEA, OLX/Jiji Africa/Asia/LatAm) cover almost every reference country. PHYSICAL STEP: photographing and shipping items.
