## How it works

Online language schools such as Lingoda sell subscriptions and need certified teachers to run scheduled Zoom classes from their CEFR-aligned materials. You choose time slots, the school fills them, and you teach without writing a curriculum or finding students. Unlike Preply or italki you don't set prices or compete on a marketplace. Lingoda wants C2 language level, a teaching certificate and 2-3 years of experience, and runs on freelance contracts.
