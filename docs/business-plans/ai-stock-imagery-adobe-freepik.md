## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$30 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $800 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **5/10**. Expect the first payment after 30–120 d.

## How it works

Adobe Stock and Freepik accept generative-AI submissions if they are labeled as AI, contain no real people/brands/artist names, and meet quality rules. An agent can research high-demand gaps (search trends, seasonal themes), generate batches, upscale, write titles/keywords and upload via FTP/CSV. Buyers (designers, marketers) license assets; you receive a royalty per download. Volume and keyword quality drive income; acceptance rates for AI content are strict.

**Platforms:** Adobe Stock Contributor, Freepik Contributor

Legal; must tick 'Created using generative AI tools', avoid real persons/trademarks/copyrighted characters and artist names in prompts.

- **Compliance requirements:** report income for tax; AI disclosure on every asset; generator ToS must grant commercial rights; no IP-infringing prompts
- **Platform availability:** Adobe Stock contributors worldwide with PayPal/Skrill; Freepik via PayPal/Payoneer, which cover most reference countries.
