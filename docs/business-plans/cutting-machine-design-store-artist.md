## How it works

Silhouette's Design Store sells cut files directly inside Silhouette Studio, which every Silhouette Cameo/Portrait owner uses. Approved artists (500+ listed) upload original designs; the store pays royalties per purchase/subscription use. Silhouette also advertises an open 'Marketplace Shop' where registered sellers list products. This reaches buyers at the point of cutting, unlike Etsy. Similar license deals exist with Cricut's Design Space image library on a contract basis.

**Platforms:** Silhouette Design Store, Cricut Design Space (licensing contracts)
