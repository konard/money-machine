## How it works

Inventors, product firms, architects and developers pay for drafting and photoreal renders, via Cad Crowd (vetted; contests, private and hourly jobs) and Upwork. Free tools (FreeCAD/Blender) plus AI textures, upscaling and scripting save hours, but accurate modeling is skilled work. Stamped drawings need licensed professionals; you deliver drafting and visuals only.

**Platforms:** Cad Crowd, Upwork, Fiverr

## Payout path

- **Methods:** Cad Crowd -> PayPal (USD) -> bank; Payoneer only arranged manually via support, Upwork -> bank/Payoneer, Fiverr -> PayPal/Payoneer
- **Minimum payout:** Cad Crowd: none stated; Fiverr $1 PayPal / $20 Payoneer (unconfirmed)
- **Frequency:** on request after security hold (Cad Crowd fixed-rate 5 days; hourly billed weekly)
- **Typical delay:** 14 days
- **Notes:** Cad Crowd: 20% service fee on hourly and fixed-rate job earnings; no fee on contest prizes. PayPal is the default payout (name must match verified profile), usually available 1 business day after request. Fixed-rate milestones have a 5-day security hold; hourly funds become withdrawable about 10 days after the billing week ends. Upwork: variable 0-15% freelancer service fee set per contract since May 2025 (pre-May-2025 contracts 10%), per Upwork's SEC 10-Q; Upwork help pages block automated fetch. Fiverr: 20% seller fee and 14-day clearance (7 days for Top Rated) are widely reported but Fiverr's help center blocked fetch, so not confirmed from the official page.

## Legality & compliance

Legal; taxable. Sealed engineering/architectural drawings require a locally licensed engineer or architect; drafting support does not.

- **Compliance requirements:** tax reporting; platform ID check; platform AI/misrepresentation rules; no stamping or implying licensure; client IP assignment/NDA
- **Platform availability:** Cad Crowd states no country restriction, but pays via PayPal, which cannot receive payments in some reference countries (e.g. Bangladesh); there Payoneer must be arranged manually. Upwork/Fiverr cover nearly all reference countries.
