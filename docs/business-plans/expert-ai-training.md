## How it works

Labs need difficult, verifiable tasks written by experts. These platforms screen for credentials, then pay hourly for authoring and grading tasks in your specialty. Rates are far above generalist work: Alignerr lists $40-120/hour. Work comes in project bursts. AI help is usually restricted, and your domain judgment is what you are paid for.

Legal everywhere. Independent-contractor status applies.

- **Platform availability:** Usually open globally, but English-language and US/EU-credential projects dominate. Each project may restrict countries.
