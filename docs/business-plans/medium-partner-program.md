## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$15 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $1,500 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **4/10**. Expect the first payment after 30–120 d.

## How it works

You write long-form articles in a niche, put them behind Medium's metered paywall, and get paid from a pool funded by member subscriptions based on member reading time, claps, highlights and follows. Distribution in Medium publications and boosts raise earnings. Medium's policy bars fully AI-generated writing from the paywall; AI may assist but must be disclosed, so this is AI-assisted rather than AI-written.
