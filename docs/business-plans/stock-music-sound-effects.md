## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$30 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $2,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **3/10**. Expect the first payment after 30–365 d.

## How it works

You compose/record royalty-free tracks or SFX packs and upload them to Pond5 (30% royalty on music/SFX, exclusive or not) and Envato's AudioJungle (flat 50% author fee for all authors since July 1, 2026, with the exclusive/non-exclusive distinction removed). Buyers are YouTubers, ad agencies and game devs who license per-use. Large catalogs earn small but steady sales. Pond5 does not accept AI-generated content, and Envato bans AI-generated content as a standalone item or primary component; human-composed work is required.

- **Minimum payout:** Pond5 $25; Envato $50

- **Typical delay:** 45 days
- **Notes:** Both pay on the 15th of the month if the balance meets the threshold; below-threshold balances roll over.
