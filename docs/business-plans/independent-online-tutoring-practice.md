## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$500 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $5,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **5/10**. Expect the first payment after 14–90 d.

## How it works

Instead of paying a marketplace commission, you get students through a Google Business Profile, a simple site, school/parent networks and referrals. Then you sell lesson packages through a booking tool (Cal.com/Calendly or tutoring software like TutorBird/Teachworks) with Stripe or PayPal checkout. Packages paid in advance make income steady and cut no-shows. Marketplaces can still be a lead source, but repeat students stay direct where terms allow. Note that Preply and italki forbid moving their students off-platform.

**Platforms:** Stripe, PayPal, Cal.com/Calendly, Google Business Profile

### Pricing strategy

Start near the local marketplace median with a free 20-minute consultation, sell prepaid 5- and 10-lesson packages, and raise rates for new students every 6 months once the schedule is full.

### Path to profit

1. Month 0: site, booking page, Google profile, tell school and parent networks (cost $20)
2. Month 1-2: first 2-3 students via referrals, $150-400/month
3. Month 3-6: 6-10 regular students, $400-1,000/month
4. Month 6-12: waitlist, higher rates, small groups; $500-2,000/month

## Legality & compliance

Legal everywhere. You may need to register as a sole trader; some countries require child-safeguarding checks for tutoring minors.

- **Compliance requirements:** self-employment/tax registration; safeguarding for minors; consumer refund terms; do not poach students from marketplaces that forbid it
- **Platform availability:** Stripe is unavailable in parts of the reference list (e.g. several African and South Asian countries); PayPal/Wise cover most of the gaps.

## Risks

- Slow client acquisition without a marketplace
- Chargebacks and no-shows
- You handle all admin
