## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$20–$100 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $400 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **7/10**. Expect the first payment after 1–22 d.

## How it works

Researchers post studies with a set reward. Studies that match your profile appear first come, first served. Prolific enforces a minimum hourly reward for researchers. Bots and AI-written answers get you banned. Amazon MTurk permanently closed on 2026-09-30, so these platforms are the main remaining option.

**Platforms:** Prolific, CloudResearch Connect

### Pricing strategy

Rates are set by researchers; maximise effective pay by taking studies above £9/$12 per hour, keeping a near-perfect approval rate and a complete profile so you qualify for higher-paying longitudinal and AI-task studies.

### Path to profit

1. Month 0: join Prolific (waitlist) and Connect, complete every profile question, link PayPal (cost $0)
2. Month 1-2: once admitted, about 1 hour/day yields $30-80/month
3. Month 3-6: keep approval near 100%, use alerts to catch studies first, unlock AI-task and longitudinal studies: $80-150/month
4. Month 6-12: plateau around $100-300/month; add paid research interviews for higher per-hour sessions

## Payout path

- **Methods:** Prolific: PayPal -> bank, Connect: PayPal, bank transfer
- **Minimum payout:** Prolific £6/$6 (combined balance); Connect $5 PayPal (max $100/transfer) or $25 bank transfer (max $250)
- **Frequency:** on demand (Prolific once per 24h; Connect once per day)
- **Typical delay:** 1 days
- **Notes:** Prolific cash-outs reach PayPal within 24 hours; researchers have up to 22 days to approve a submission, and new or changed PayPal accounts get a 72-hour cool-off. The PayPal account country must match your residence or country of birth (US residents need a US PayPal).

## Full cycle (setup → earn → bank account)

1. Join the waitlist / sign up
2. Complete the profile
3. Take studies as they appear
4. Get approved
5. Cash out to PayPal -> bank

## Legality & compliance

Taking part in research for pay is legal everywhere. Account eligibility, not legality, is the limiting factor.

- **Compliance requirements:** report income for tax; one account per person; ID/phone verification
- **Platform availability:** Prolific's help centre gates eligibility through PayPal country rules rather than publishing a country list here; third-party reports say it accepts mostly OECD countries plus South Africa and waitlists are common (country list not confirmed from an official page). Connect is US-centric.

## Risks

- Waitlists can last months
- Bans for failed attention checks
- Low study volume outside the US/UK
