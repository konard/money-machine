## How it works

Create genuinely useful tools (solar savings calculator, B2B software selector quiz, 'do I need a lawyer for X' guide) that end in a clearly consented form or partner redirect. CPA/lead networks (e.g. MaxBounty) or vendor partner programs pay a fixed amount per qualified lead or signup ($5-$100+ depending on vertical). Incentivized or fake form fills are fraud.

**Platforms:** MaxBounty, CJ/Impact CPL offers, vendor partner programs (B2B SaaS)

## Legality & compliance

Lead gen is legal broadly; insurance, legal-referral and credit leads are regulated, so the score assumes unregulated verticals (B2B, solar, home services).

- **Restricted or banned in:** Insurance/legal lead referral needs a licence in some US states and EU countries
- **Compliance requirements:** TCPA/GDPR explicit consent for contact; no incentivized or fake submissions; affiliate disclosure (FTC/CAP); vertical licensing where required
- **Platform availability:** CPA networks focus on US/UK/CA/AU traffic; B2B vendor programs accept affiliates globally.

## Risks

- Lead scrubbing and chargebacks
- Strict network approval
- Regulatory fines for consent failures (TCPA)
- Offer caps/pauses
