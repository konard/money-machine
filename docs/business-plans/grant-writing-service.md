## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$400 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $6,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **4/10**. Expect the first payment after 14–120 d.

## How it works

Nonprofits, labs and startups pay because applications are long and deadline-driven. You track public RFPs, use an LLM to draft narratives from the client's own facts, and edit to each funder's rubric. Charge per application or retainer, not a % of the award (commission pricing is considered unethical and often barred from grant funds). Clients reapply every cycle.

**Platforms:** Upwork, Fiverr, direct (Candid/Grants.gov research + Stripe/Wise invoices)

## Legality & compliance

Legal everywhere; taxable. Never fabricate data, outcomes or letters of support; the applicant signs and is responsible for submissions.

- **Compliance requirements:** tax reporting; platform ID check; platform AI/misrepresentation rules; flat/hourly fees, no award contingency (GPA/AFP ethics-code norm; both sites blocked fetch, unconfirmed); truthful applications only
- **Platform availability:** Upwork/Fiverr pay out in nearly all reference countries via Payoneer/bank.

## Risks

- awards outside your control drive client churn
- low-budget nonprofits haggle on price
- AI-sounding generic drafts get rejected
