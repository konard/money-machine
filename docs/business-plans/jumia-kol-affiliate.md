## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$30 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $500 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **3/10**. Expect the first payment after 30–120 d.

## How it works

Jumia, Africa's largest e-commerce marketplace, replaced its affiliate program with the KOL program in 2020. Creators, bloggers and WhatsApp/Telegram deal channels share tracked links. Commission varies by category (fashion higher, electronics lower) and is paid after the return window. One of the few affiliate programs paying in local African currency.
