## How it works

You build Lightroom presets (.xmp) or video LUTs (.cube) for specific looks (film, wedding, travel) and sell packs on Etsy, Gumroad, Payhip or Creative Market. Buyers pay to save editing time and match a style. Before/after previews are the main selling tool. The niche is saturated, so a specific niche or bundling with your own photo/video audience helps.

**Platforms:** Etsy, Gumroad, Payhip, Creative Market
