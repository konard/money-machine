## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$300 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $4,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **5/10**. Expect the first payment after 14–90 d.

## How it works

You list a class (one-off, multi-week, ongoing weekly or self-paced) on a topic kids or homeschooling parents want, set the price and group size, and teach live over Outschool's video tool. Parents find classes through Outschool search and pay up front. Outschool takes a 30% teacher fee and pays you 70%; parents also pay a separate marketplace fee. Ongoing weekly classes create recurring income once a class gets reviews.

**Platforms:** Outschool

### Pricing strategy

Launch at or slightly below the category median (about $10-12 per meeting) with a one-time intro class to collect reviews, then raise to $15-20 and push learners into ongoing weekly classes.

## Legality & compliance

Teaching children online is legal everywhere; child-safeguarding rules and Outschool's background check apply.

- **Compliance requirements:** Outschool background check / identity verification; child-safeguarding and privacy rules; report income for tax
- **Platform availability:** Teachers must be full-time residents of the US, Mexico, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, South Korea, Spain or the UK. Of the 50 reference countries only US, CA, MX, UK, AU and KR qualify (about 12%).
