## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$-200–$0 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $5,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **4/10**. Expect the first payment after 14–120 d.

## How it works

You build a Shopify (or WooCommerce) store, import products from AliExpress, CJ Dropshipping, Zendrop or print-on-demand suppliers like Printful, and drive traffic with paid ads, TikTok or SEO. When a customer orders, you pay the supplier who ships directly. Honest expectations: most new stores lose money on ads and close within months; profitable operators typically test many products and have strong creative.

**Platforms:** Shopify, AliExpress, CJ Dropshipping, Printful, Meta/TikTok Ads

Winners are operators with strong creative testing, branded products and faster shipping (US/EU warehouses), not generic AliExpress stores. Rising ad costs, de minimis import changes in the US (2025) and shoppers comparing on Temu/TikTok Shop squeeze margins. Most beginners burn their ad budget on product tests and quit.

### Pricing strategy

Price at 2.5-3x landed cost so ad cost per purchase fits; raise prices with bundles and branding once a product converts.

### Path to profit

1. Month 0: build store with AI copy, pick 3-5 test products ($300 incl. first ads)
2. Month 1-2: ad tests, mostly losses (-$100 to -$300)
3. Month 3-6: kill losers, scale one winner if found; move to faster-shipping supplier
4. Month 6-12: either $500+/month on a winner or close the store

## Payout path

- **Methods:** Shopify Payments -> bank, PayPal, Stripe
- **Minimum payout:** No minimum
- **Frequency:** Shopify Payments every 1-3 business days
- **Typical delay:** 3 days
- **Notes:** PayPal/Stripe may hold funds for new merchants with long delivery times.

## Full cycle (setup → earn → bank account)

1. Create Shopify store
2. Import products
3. Run ads/content
4. Orders auto-forwarded to supplier
5. Payouts to bank

- Store/payment KYC

## Legality & compliance

Legal everywhere; consumer law applies (delivery times, EU 14-day refunds, GPSR, no counterfeits).

- **Compliance requirements:** business/tax registration as revenue grows; VAT/sales tax; consumer-protection and refund compliance; ad platform policies
- **Platform availability:** Shopify sells in 175+ countries; Shopify Payments works in ~23 countries, elsewhere PayPal/Stripe/local gateways. Suppliers ship globally.

## Risks

- Ad spend losses (most common outcome)
- Long shipping -> chargebacks and payment holds
- Supplier quality issues
- Ad account bans

## Evidence that people get paid

Shopify hosts millions of merchants; industry surveys commonly estimate only ~10-20% of dropshipping stores become profitable.
