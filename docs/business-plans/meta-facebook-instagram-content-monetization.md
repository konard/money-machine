## How it works

Meta merged in-stream ads, ads on Reels and Performance bonus into a single Facebook Content Monetization program. A professional-mode profile or Page posting original content is invited (Meta's help page says the program is currently invitation-only; you can register interest in the Monetization tab), and earns a share of ad revenue plus bonuses by engagement. Instagram pays via Gifts on Reels, Subscriptions and invite-only bonuses. Meta's 2025 crackdown on 'unoriginal content' removes reach and monetization from reposted/aggregated material, so content must be your own.

**Platforms:** Facebook, Instagram, Meta Business Suite
