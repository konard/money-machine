Most people who try this earn around **$10–$60 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $400 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **7/10**. Expect the first payment after 3–30 d.

## How it works

Companies pay UX research platforms to recruit testers who match a demographic. You take a screener, and if you qualify you record a 10-20 minute screen-and-voice session, usually paid about $10, while live interviews pay $30-120. Screeners are competitive, so most of the time goes into qualifying. The feedback has to be genuinely yours.

**Platforms:** UserTesting, Userlytics, Trymata (now QuestionPro UX)

## Legality & compliance

Legal everywhere.

- **Compliance requirements:** report income for tax; verified PayPal; screen/voice recording consent
- **Platform availability:** UserTesting recruits only in countries where PayPal operates. Its standard tester countries are the US, UK, DE, FR, CA, IN and AU. Userlytics recruits more broadly.

## Risks

- Few tests outside core countries
- Ratings drop if audio quality is poor

## Evidence that people get paid

UserTesting's official 'get paid to test' page says PayPal is the only method and pays ~14 days after completion. Userlytics' tester page lists $10-50 per study, paid every 15 days. Reviews consistently report $10 per standard UserTesting test.
