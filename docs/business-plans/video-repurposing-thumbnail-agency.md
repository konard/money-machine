## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$600 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $8,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **5/10**. Expect the first payment after 7–60 d.

## How it works

Creators, coaches and B2B companies with long-form video pay for consistent output. AI clipping finds highlights, auto-captions and reframes to vertical; you finish edits, pacing and thumbnails. Sell monthly packages (e.g. 4 long edits + 20 shorts). You only edit footage the client owns; posting happens from the client's accounts.

**Platforms:** Upwork, Fiverr, Contra, direct
