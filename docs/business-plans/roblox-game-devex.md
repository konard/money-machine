## How it works

You build a game in Roblox Studio (Luau scripting) and monetize with game passes, developer products, UGC items and Premium Payouts. Players spend Robux; earned Robux can be exchanged via the Developer Exchange (DevEx) at $0.0038 per Robux once you hold 30,000+ earned Robux (~$114). Success is hit-driven; most games earn nothing, a few earn millions.

**Platforms:** Roblox, Roblox DevEx

## Payout path

- **Methods:** DevEx via Tipalti -> bank/PayPal
- **Minimum payout:** 30,000 earned Robux (~$114 at the standard rate)
- **Frequency:** one cash-out request per calendar month
- **Typical delay:** 14 days
- **Notes:** Requires age 13+, verified email, DevEx portal (Tipalti) account and W-8/W-9. Standard rate $0.0038/Robux; $0.0054/Robux for certain purchases in eligible games by ID/face-verified US players aged 18+; Robux earned before Sept 5, 2025 cash out at $0.0035. Processing ~10 business days first time, ~5 thereafter, plus bank time.

## Legality & compliance

Legal; Roblox itself has been blocked by some governments (platform availability, not illegality of the activity).

- **Compliance requirements:** report income for tax; DevEx terms and tax forms; child-safety and content policies
- **Platform availability:** DevEx available in most countries except sanctioned ones; Roblox access has been blocked in a few countries at times.
