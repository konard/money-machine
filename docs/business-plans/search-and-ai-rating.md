## How it works

Search and AI companies outsource quality rating to vendors. You pass a guideline exam, often unpaid and multi-part, then work 10-20 hours a week rating tasks. Pay is set per country, around $12-14/hour in the US and as low as $5/hour in some Asian markets. Quality audits decide whether you keep access. The work cannot be automated.

**Platforms:** TELUS Digital AI Community, RWS TrainAI
