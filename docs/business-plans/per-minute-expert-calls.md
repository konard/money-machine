## How it works

You list your expertise (marketing, fundraising, SaaS, engineering) on Clarity.fm and set an hourly rate that is displayed per minute. Callers are billed per minute; Clarity keeps 15%. Bookings come from Clarity search and, more often, from links you place on your site or social profiles.
