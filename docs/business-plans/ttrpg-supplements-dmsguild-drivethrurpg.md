## How it works

On DMs Guild you may use official D&D IP (Forgotten Realms etc.) under the Community Content Program and earn a reported 50% royalty. On DriveThruRPG you sell your own-IP or 5e-SRD (CC-BY) products for a higher reported share. Customers are GMs looking for ready-to-run content; bestseller medals (Copper/Silver/etc.) drive visibility. Bundles and print-on-demand versions add revenue. Text must be human-written: the marketplaces do not accept products written primarily by AI.

**Platforms:** DMs Guild, DriveThruRPG
