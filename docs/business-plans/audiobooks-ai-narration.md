## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$15 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $1,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **4/10**. Expect the first payment after 45–270 d.

## How it works

If you own ebook rights, you can create audio editions: Amazon KDP Virtual Voice (AI, US, invite/beta), Google Play Books auto-narrated audiobooks (English, Spanish, French, German, Hindi, Brazilian Portuguese; free "for a limited time"), and Voices by INaudio (formerly Findaway Voices) which distributes AI-narrated audio with disclosure to many retailers. ACX/Audible proper still requires human narration (or the narrator's own Voice Replica). Retail buyers and subscription services pay; distributors pass royalties monthly.
