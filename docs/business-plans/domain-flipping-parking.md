## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$-50–$0 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $3,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **2/10**. Expect the first payment after 90–720 d.

## How it works

You register available domains (~$10/year) or buy expiring ones, list them with Buy-Now prices on Afternic (GoDaddy network; Dan.com is being folded into it) and Sedo, and wait for an end user to buy. Annual sell-through for typical portfolios is ~0.5-2%, so you need hundreds of names and years of renewals; a single sale can be $500-$5,000. Parking revenue has collapsed (Google changed AdSense-for-domains defaults), so treat parking as negligible.

**Platforms:** Afternic, Sedo, Atom, GoDaddy Auctions, NameBio (comps)

### Pricing strategy

List with Buy Now prices in the $1,000-$3,000 band for brandables (fast lane on Afternic), accept offers above 3x acquisition cost, and drop names that get no inquiries after two renewals.

## Legality & compliance

Domain trading is legal everywhere; registering names containing others' trademarks is cybersquatting (UDRP/ACPA) and must be avoided.

- **Compliance requirements:** trademark screening; registrar KYC; tax on sale gains
- **Platform availability:** Registrars and marketplaces accept customers from nearly all reference countries; payouts via wire, PayPal or Payoneer.

## Risks

- Renewal costs exceed sales
- Trademark disputes
- Very long time to first sale
- Market saturated with AI-generated names

## Evidence that people get paid

NameBio records thousands of public sales weekly; established investors report 1% annual sell-through with 10-30x markup.
