## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$5–$30 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $150 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **9/10**. Expect the first payment after 3–21 d.

## How it works

Market-research firms pay panel members for opinions. Each survey pays roughly $0.50–$5 and takes 5–20 minutes. Joining 3–5 panels at once keeps a steady flow of qualifying surveys. Balances are cashed out to PayPal (or bank on some panels) once a low minimum is reached, typically $5–$10. This is labor, not passive income, and the hourly rate is poor, but the payment itself is reliable.

**Platforms:** Swagbucks, Branded Surveys, Toluna, YouGov, PrizeRebel, Survey Junkie, Mobrog

## Payout path

- **Methods:** PayPal, bank transfer (some panels), gift cards
- **Minimum payout:** $5–$10 depending on panel
- **Frequency:** on request
- **Typical delay:** 3 days
- **Notes:** Branded Surveys (official site): 500 points = $5 minimum, via PayPal, bank transfer or Tango gift cards. Swagbucks (help centre): PayPal cash arrives in 2–5 business days; the first PayPal redemption needs matching names and a confirmed bank on PayPal; the SB minimum was not stated on the pages checked. PrizeRebel (official site): PayPal, Bitcoin and gift cards; minimum not confirmed. YouGov/Toluna: PayPal/vouchers shown, thresholds not confirmed on official pages.

## What can be automated

- Survey answering must stay manual (ToS).
- Automate reminders: a daily script that lists new surveys from panel email notifications.
- Automate payout logging: parse PayPal payout emails into the earnings ledger.

## Unavoidable manual steps

- Sign up for 3–5 panels with the same email and PayPal
- Fill profile questionnaires fully (they drive survey invites)
- Take surveys; cash out at minimum

## Legality & compliance

Legal everywhere in the reference set. Panels ban multiple accounts, VPN use and automated answering.

- **Compliance requirements:** one account per person; truthful demographic profile; no VPN or automation (ToS)
- **Platform availability:** Swagbucks help centre: live in most countries in the Americas, Europe and Asia and some in Africa. Branded Surveys: US, Canada and UK residents only (official). Toluna and YouGov run country panels in many markets. Survey Junkie is reported as US/Canada/Australia only (not re-checked). Mobrog runs in 70+ countries with a €5 minimum paid via PayPal/Skrill (unverified).

## Risks

- Low hourly rate ($2–$6 effective)
- Frequent disqualification mid-survey
- Account bans for inconsistent answers

## Evidence that people get paid

Swagbucks publicly states it has paid members over $1 billion in rewards; YouGov, Toluna and Branded Surveys are long-running panels owned by established market-research groups.
