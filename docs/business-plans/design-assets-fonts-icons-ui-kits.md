## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$60 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $4,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **3/10**. Expect the first payment after 30–240 d.

## How it works

You produce a polished asset pack and list it on Creative Market (shop application with portfolio) or UI8 (curated). Designers and agencies buy commercial licenses. Creative Market's commission varies per shop/product (not published) and it pays monthly via Tipalti with a $20 minimum. Figma Community is not approving new creators to sell paid files (15% fee for existing sellers), so sell Figma kits via these marketplaces or Gumroad. Fonts need real type-design skill; icons/UI kits are faster with AI-assisted ideation.

**Platforms:** Creative Market, UI8, Gumroad
