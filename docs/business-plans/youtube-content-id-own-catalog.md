## How it works

You release original music through a distributor that offers Content ID (DistroKid's annual per-release 'Social Media Pack' extra, CD Baby Social Video Monetization). YouTube fingerprints the audio and, when any uploader's video uses it, monetizes that video and routes a share of ad revenue to you. Works best for tracks creators reuse (lo-fi, stream background music). Only recordings you own exclusively qualify.

**Platforms:** YouTube Content ID, DistroKid, CD Baby

## Legality & compliance

Claiming content you exclusively own is legal; claiming public-domain, royalty-free or others' material violates YouTube policy and can be fraud.

- **Compliance requirements:** exclusive rights to entire recording (no samples, loops from libraries or public-domain elements); distributor KYC/tax form
- **Platform availability:** DistroKid and CD Baby accept artists worldwide; YouTube ads run globally.

## Risks

- Low revenue unless tracks are widely reused
- Disputes/reputation damage if claims hit licensed users
- Incompatible with non-exclusive stock licensing of the same track
- Distributor may remove ineligible tracks permanently

## Evidence that people get paid

DistroKid's official help article documents Content ID via the Social Media Pack with a 20% cut; Content ID is a standard monetization line in distributor statements.
