## How it works

Blue Mountain Arts buys contemporary prose or poems of 50-300 words, written from personal experience, for greeting cards and gift products. You submit by email or mail by seasonal deadlines. Accepted pieces earn $300 for worldwide exclusive card and product rights, or $50 for one-time book use. Rhymed, religious, one-liner and AI-generated or AI-assisted work is refused, and pieces not selected get no reply.
