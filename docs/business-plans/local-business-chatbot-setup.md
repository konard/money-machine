## How it works

Clinics, salons, trades and restaurants pay to answer FAQs and capture bookings 24/7. You ingest their site/FAQ into a no-code bot builder, connect booking or CRM, test and install. Monthly fee covers hosting, updates and reports. Leads come from local networking, Upwork and compliant outreach.

**Platforms:** Upwork, Fiverr, direct sales (Stripe invoices)

Interest in AI receptionists and chatbots for local businesses is growing, but the offer is heavily marketed by course-sellers and SaaS vendors now sell self-serve versions directly to SMBs. Getting paid depends almost entirely on local selling; churn is high when bots don't produce bookings. Fixed monthly tool costs make it loss-making until 1 client signs.

### Pricing strategy

Offer a free 14-day pilot on the client's real FAQ, then charge a modest setup fee plus monthly management tied to measured bookings or leads handled.

### Path to profit

1. Month 0: build 2 demo bots (salon, clinic), recorded demo videos (cost $50)
2. Month 1-3: 20-50 local conversations to close the first client, expected $0-$300
3. Month 3-6: case study, referrals from the first clients, 2-4 clients
4. Month 6-12: 5-15 clients on retainer for those who keep selling

## Legality & compliance

Legal everywhere; bots must disclose they are AI where required (e.g. EU AI Act transparency), and health/legal businesses need careful scoping (no medical/legal advice).

- **Compliance requirements:** AI disclosure to end users; privacy policy for collected data; anti-spam compliant outreach
- **Platform availability:** Direct invoicing via Stripe is unavailable in some reference countries (e.g. Nigeria, Bangladesh); use Payoneer/Wise or Upwork there.
