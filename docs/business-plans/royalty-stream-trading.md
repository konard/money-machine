## How it works

Royalty Exchange and SongVest run auctions and offerings where owners sell a share or term of their royalty income (music publishing, master, book, film). Sellers need at least three years of royalty statements; Royalty Exchange charges no listing fee and takes a commission agreed before listing (rate not published; third parties report ~12-15%). Buyers receive royalty distributions in proportion to the share bought. SongVest sells fractional 'SongShares' under SEC Regulation A. Without royalties you can only participate as an investor (capital, decay risk).

**Platforms:** Royalty Exchange, SongVest

## Payout path

- **Methods:** bank transfer (ACH/wire)
- **Minimum payout:** Auction proceeds; investor distributions with no stated minimum
- **Frequency:** per sale / quarterly distributions
- **Typical delay:** 60 days
- **Notes:** Royalty Exchange buyers wire funds into escrow within two business days of signing; funds are released to the seller after rights transfer. Investors are paid as the platform collects royalties. Seller commission and investor minimums are not published (unverified).

## Legality & compliance

Royalty contracts are legal everywhere; buying foreign assets may need approval under capital controls in the 5 listed countries (5/50 = 10% restricted, so 90%).

- **Restricted or banned in:** Bangladesh, Nepal, Tunisia, Morocco, Libya
- **Compliance requirements:** KYC/AML; tax on royalty income and sale proceeds; clear rights ownership documentation
- **Platform availability:** US-based platforms; international users can participate subject to KYC and US tax forms. Sellers need existing royalty assets.
