## How it works

Home sewists buy PDF patterns that include tiled A4/Letter pages, A0 copy-shop files, layered sizes and projector files, plus an illustrated instruction booklet. Quilters buy block/quilt patterns with fabric requirements and cutting charts. Etsy and Payhip handle direct sales (Payhip free plan: 5% fee, sales deposited instantly to your PayPal/Stripe). Makerist, a European pattern marketplace, appeared offline in Nov 2026. Pattern testers and sew-along posts drive launches; one pattern sells for years.
