## How it works

You write a technical book (programming, DevOps, data) in Markdown, publish early, and update it as you go so buyers get every new version. Readers pay a minimum price you set, can pay more, and get a 60-day refund. Leanpub pays 80% of purchases from $7.99, or 80% minus 50 cents below that. Buyers are developers who want depth that blog posts lack, so the book needs real expertise. AI can help with code samples and editing, but the author has to check every example.
