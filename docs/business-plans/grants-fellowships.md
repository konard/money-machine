## How it works

Funders award non-dilutive grants for open-source maintenance or ambitious individual projects. FLOSS/fund (Zerodha, up to $1M/yr) gives $10k-$100k per project per year to established projects that publish a funding.json manifest, reviewed quarterly; NLnet gives EUR 5k-50k first grants via recurring calls (Restack, CodeSupply open with a 3 Nov 2026 deadline) to projects with a strong European dimension; Sovereign Tech Agency funds critical OSS infrastructure globally (Fund minimum EUR 50k of work) plus Fellowship/Standards programs; Emergent Ventures offers micro-grants. Payment is by bank transfer after selection and contracting.
