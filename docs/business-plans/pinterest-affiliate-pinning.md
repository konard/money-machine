## How it works

Pinterest is a search engine for planning (home decor, recipes, crafts, fashion). You design original pins with keyword-rich titles that link either directly to affiliate products (allowed with disclosure) or to your own blog posts containing affiliate links, which is safer and diversifies. Pins have long shelf lives, so a consistent library compounds traffic. Payers are the merchants/networks behind the links (Amazon, LTK, Awin, etc.).

**Platforms:** Pinterest, Amazon Associates/Awin/Impact/LTK, Tailwind (scheduler)
