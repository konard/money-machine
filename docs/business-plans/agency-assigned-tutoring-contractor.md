## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$100–$600 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $2,500 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **7/10**. Expect the first payment after 14–60 d.

## How it works

Tutoring companies sell lesson subscriptions to parents and assign the students to freelance tutors. You don't market yourself or set prices. You take students in your subjects, teach on the company's classroom tool and log sessions. GoStudent (Europe) pays a base rate of up to GBP 16 per 50-minute lesson, with volume raises (+1.00 per lesson after 150 lessons in 6 months, +1.50 after 300). Varsity Tutors (US) reportedly pays per session twice a week (third-party reports, unverified).

**Platforms:** GoStudent, Varsity Tutors

## Payout path

- **Methods:** transfer to bank account (GoStudent), direct deposit (Varsity Tutors, unverified)
- **Minimum payout:** No minimum (GoStudent); Varsity Tutors not stated
- **Frequency:** monthly (GoStudent) / twice weekly (Varsity)
- **Typical delay:** 30 days
- **Notes:** GoStudent's tutor help center: base rate up to GBP 16 per 50-minute session, depending on availability, subjects and monthly sessions completed. Earnings are paid in full the next month with no minimum, between the 12th and 17th; transfers can take up to 7 days to arrive. Session feedback must be submitted within 24 hours or the session is not paid. No-shows pay a flat 6.50. Volume raises: +1.00 per lesson after 150 lessons in 6 months, +1.50 after 300. Varsity Tutors' twice-weekly pay and USD 15-40/hr come only from third-party sources; its tutor pages block automated fetches.

## Legality & compliance

Legal everywhere; you are an independent contractor and handle your own tax.

- **Compliance requirements:** contractor tax registration; background check for minors; subject test and onboarding
- **Platform availability:** GoStudent runs sites for the UK, DE, AT, FR, IT, ES, TR, PL, NL and GR and says anyone over 18 can apply, but it names no residence rule. Varsity Tutors contracts with US residents only and reportedly excludes several US states (unverified).

## Risks
