## How it works

Two payers: (1) Canva Creators program pays monthly royalties from a pool based on how often Pro users use your templates/elements (application with portfolio required; element sign-ups were on hold); (2) buyers on Etsy, Creative Market or Gumroad pay for a PDF containing a Canva template link (social posts, resumes, media kits). Canva pays through Trolley.

**Platforms:** Canva Creators, Etsy, Creative Market
