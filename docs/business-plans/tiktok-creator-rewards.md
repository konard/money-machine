## How it works

Creators 18+ with 10,000 followers and 100,000 views in the last 30 days, in an eligible country, join Creator Rewards. TikTok pays from its own program budget for 'qualified views' on original videos over 60 seconds; RPM depends on originality, watch time, search value and engagement. AI-generated content must carry TikTok's AI label, and unoriginal/reposted content earns nothing.

**Platforms:** TikTok
