## How it works

You create a downloadable product (PDF guide, template pack, code kit) and list it on Gumroad, Payhip or Lemon Squeezy. They handle checkout and VAT (Gumroad and Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record). Buyers are your own audience (X, Reddit, newsletter, SEO blog). Fees: Gumroad 10% + $0.50 on direct sales (30% via Discover); Payhip Free plan 5% (Plus $29/mo 2%, Pro $99/mo 0%) plus PayPal/Stripe fees; Lemon Squeezy 5% + $0.50.

### Path to profit

## Payout path

- **Methods:** Gumroad: local bank deposit (~120 countries) or PayPal where bank payouts are unsupported (2% fee); no Payoneer/Wise, Payhip: paid instantly to your PayPal or Stripe account, Lemon Squeezy: bank transfer or PayPal (USD)
- **Minimum payout:** Gumroad $100 for weekly/monthly/quarterly payouts (higher local minimums in some countries; instant payouts from $1 for eligible accounts); Payhip none (instant); Lemon Squeezy $50
- **Frequency:** weekly by default (Gumroad, 7-day hold), instant (Payhip), twice-monthly on the 1st and 15th (Lemon Squeezy, 13-day hold)
- **Typical delay:** 7 days
- **Notes:** Gumroad's help center states a $100 USD minimum balance for all scheduled payouts (not only unverified accounts); balances below roll over. Lemon Squeezy (owned by Stripe) is building a Stripe Managed Payments merchant-of-record product, currently waitlist-only; Lemon Squeezy itself still accepts sign-ups.

## Legality & compliance

Selling self-made information products is legal everywhere in the reference set; platforms collect VAT as merchant of record.

- **Compliance requirements:** report income for tax; KYC/identity verification; respect copyright of included material
- **Platform availability:** Gumroad pays to local banks in ~120 countries (incl. most reference countries such as India, Nigeria, Kenya, Philippines, Indonesia, Mexico) and via PayPal elsewhere; Payhip pays wherever PayPal/Stripe can receive; Lemon Squeezy pays to banks or PayPal in its supported countries. Countries where PayPal cannot receive (e.g. Lebanon, Iraq, Libya) are poorly served.

## Risks

- No built-in traffic; sales depend on audience
- Gumroad's $100 payout minimum delays the first payout for small sellers (use Payhip for instant payouts)
- Lemon Squeezy transition toward Stripe Managed Payments
- Low-quality AI products get refunds/chargebacks

## Evidence that people get paid

Gumroad has paid creators hundreds of millions cumulatively.
