## How it works

Consumers commission personalized songs for birthdays and weddings through marketplaces (e.g. Fiverr's music categories, Songfinch artist roster), and small businesses and podcasters buy jingles, sonic logos and intros. You take a brief, write lyrics (LLM-assisted drafts), record and deliver a mixed track with a usage license. Short formats make turnaround fast.

**Platforms:** Fiverr, Upwork, Songfinch (artist roster), direct (Stripe/PayPal invoices)

### Pricing strategy

Start with a $50-80 package (60-second song, 3-day delivery) to get reviews, then raise to $150+ and sell add-ons (full length, instrumental, rush).

### Path to profit

1. Month 0: 3 sample songs and gig listings (cost $0)
2. Month 1-2: first 2-3 orders, $100-200
3. Month 3-6: seasonal peaks (Valentine's, weddings) plus business jingles; $200-600/month
4. Month 6-12: higher tiers and direct clients; $300-1,500/month for active sellers

## Legality & compliance

Legal everywhere; taxable. Do not imitate a specific artist's voice or reuse copyrighted melodies/lyrics.

- **Compliance requirements:** license terms in delivery; no voice cloning of real artists without consent; platform AI-use disclosure; tax reporting
- **Platform availability:** Fiverr/Upwork pay out almost everywhere via Payoneer. Songfinch says its roster is worldwide, but how it pays non-US artists is not published.
