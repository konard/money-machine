## How it works

Patent firms and inventors need black-and-white line drawings meeting strict office rules. You turn CAD exports, photos or sketches into compliant vector figures; scripts handle numbering and sheet layout, you ensure accuracy. Firms pay per sheet and repeat. Claim drafting is regulated legal work; you only illustrate.

**Platforms:** Upwork, Fiverr, Cad Crowd, direct to IP law firms

## Legality & compliance

Legal everywhere; taxable. Patent prosecution and claim drafting are reserved to registered practitioners in many countries; drawings by draftspersons are commonly outsourced.

- **Compliance requirements:** tax reporting; platform ID check; platform AI/misrepresentation rules; confidentiality of unpublished inventions (NDA); no legal advice on patentability
- **Platform availability:** Upwork/Fiverr pay out in nearly all reference countries via Payoneer/bank.

## Risks

- office rejections if rules missed
- small niche; relationship-driven
- confidentiality liability

## Evidence that people get paid

USPTO MPEP 608 sets detailed paper/drawing standards (A4 or 8.5x11 sheets, high-contrast black lines), which is why firms outsource to specialists. Demand volume not quantified.
