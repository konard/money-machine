## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$0 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $4,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **2/10**. Expect the first payment after 180–730 d.

## How it works

Buyers pay roughly 25-45x monthly profit for established sites/newsletters. You build a niche site or newsletter (AI-assisted), grow it to verifiable revenue over 6-24 months, then list it on Flippa (small deals) or Empire Flippers (larger, vetted). Proceeds move through escrow to your bank. Alternatively buy undervalued sites and improve them, which needs capital. Starter sites without revenue sell for little.

**Platforms:** Flippa, Empire Flippers, Motion Invest, Duuce (newsletters), Escrow.com

### Pricing strategy

## Payout path

- **Methods:** Escrow.com/platform escrow -> bank wire
- **Minimum payout:** Per sale; Flippa self-service 10% success fee + listing fee from $29; Empire Flippers flat $10,000 commission up to ~$66.7k sale price, 15% up to $700k
- **Frequency:** per sale
- **Typical delay:** 30 days
- **Notes:** Escrow and asset migration typically 2-6 weeks; earn-outs possible. Empire Flippers' $10k minimum commission makes it unsuitable for small sites; use Flippa below ~$70k.

## Legality & compliance

Selling a website/digital business is legal everywhere; misrepresenting traffic or revenue is fraud.

- **Compliance requirements:** accurate financial disclosure; verified analytics; tax on capital gains/business income; platform KYC
- **Platform availability:** Flippa and Empire Flippers accept sellers from almost all countries; escrow pays by international wire.

## Risks

- Google updates crushing AI content sites
- No buyer at desired price
- Long build time
- Escrow scams outside platform
