## How it works

Approved VRChat sellers sell avatar/world unlocks and group subscriptions for VRChat Credits, and the creator keeps about 50% of what users spend (about $0.005 per credit), paid out via Tilia. Unity packages, outfits and shaders sell as downloads on Jinxxy (5% fee plus about 3% processing, weekly payouts) or on BOOTH. Compatibility with popular avatar bases drives repeat sales.

**Platforms:** VRChat Creator Economy, Tilia, Jinxxy, BOOTH (pixiv)

## Payout path

- **Methods:** VRChat: Tilia -> PayPal or bank transfer, Jinxxy: weekly payouts ($1.50 fee), BOOTH: transfer to bank (JPY)
- **Minimum payout:** VRChat: 20,000 earned credits (about $100); Jinxxy/BOOTH: not stated
- **Frequency:** on request (VRChat) / weekly (Jinxxy) / monthly (BOOTH)
- **Typical delay:** 7 days
- **Notes:** Tilia approves most VRChat payouts in 1-2 business days, and PayPal charges about 1.5%. BOOTH pays monthly from the 20th with a JPY 200-300 transfer fee. Whether BOOTH pays to non-Japanese banks is unverified.
