## How it works

You produce instrumentals in popular genres (trap, drill, afrobeats, R&B) and list them on a beat store with tiered license contracts: cheap non-exclusive leases with stream caps, pricier stem/unlimited leases, and exclusive buyouts (the same mechanic covers 'ghost production' full-rights sales to DJs). Artists pay because they need affordable, ready-made production. Traffic comes from the marketplace search plus YouTube 'type beat' uploads that link to the store. Each beat can be leased many times until sold exclusively.

**Platforms:** BeatStars, Airbit, YouTube (type-beat promotion)

## Payout path

- **Methods:** PayPal -> bank, Stripe -> bank, Tipalti (Airbit marketplace: PayPal/ACH/wire/check) -> bank
- **Minimum payout:** $0: direct store sales land in your own PayPal/Stripe; Airbit marketplace earnings have no minimum
- **Frequency:** per sale (BeatStars, Airbit personal stores); monthly for Airbit marketplace
- **Typical delay:** 3 days
- **Notes:** Verified (help centers, Nov 2026): BeatStars takes 0% of beat sales and funds go straight to the seller's connected PayPal/Stripe; a 12% Marketplace service fee is added to the buyer's price (sellers may opt to cover it), not charged on Pro Page/Blaze sales. Uploading and selling on BeatStars requires a paid Starter/Growth/Professional plan (pricing page showed Starter at $19.99/year during a promotion; free accounts are capped at 10 tracks). Airbit has a free account and Platinum ($19.99/mo or $7.99/mo billed annually); personal stores are zero-commission and pay instantly via PayPal/Stripe; marketplace earnings are paid on the last working day of the following month via Tipalti with no minimum threshold. Airbit's marketplace commission rate is not published. Airbit Content ID (Platinum only) pays 80%.

## Legality & compliance

Selling licenses to your own compositions is legal everywhere; income is taxable. Beats using uncleared samples or AI output without rights cannot be licensed.

- **Compliance requirements:** own or clear every sample/loop used; clear license terms (stream caps, credit); tax forms (W-8BEN for non-US); register works with PRO/publishing admin if desired
- **Platform availability:** Both platforms accept sellers worldwide; direct sales need a PayPal or Stripe account in the seller's country, and Airbit marketplace payouts go via Tipalti (PayPal, ACH, wire, check). PayPal receiving is limited in a few reference countries (e.g. Bangladesh, Libya, Iraq), and Stripe is unavailable in many African and South Asian countries, so Airbit's Tipalti wire option may be the only route there. Zero-capital route is Airbit's free account; BeatStars needs a paid plan (about $20/year at promo pricing).
