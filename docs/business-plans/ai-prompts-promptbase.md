## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$5 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $300 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **2/10**. Expect the first payment after 14–180 d.

## How it works

You craft prompts that reliably produce a specific output (e.g. a sticker style, a product-photo look, an SEO workflow), test them, and list on PromptBase with example outputs. Buyers pay a few dollars to skip trial-and-error. PromptBase takes a 20% fee on marketplace sales (0% on sales via your own referral link) and pays via Stripe or Zoneless (USDC). Demand has declined as models improved and prompting became easier, so income is usually small.

## Payout path

- **Methods:** Stripe Connect -> bank, Zoneless (daily USDC payout, then off-ramp to bank)
- **Minimum payout:** Stripe $30 (account must be 30+ days old); Zoneless no minimum
- **Frequency:** weekly or monthly (Stripe); daily (Zoneless); early payout fee $3.99
- **Typical delay:** 14 days
- **Notes:** Stripe transfer to bank can take 3-5 days after release. Zoneless pays in a crypto stablecoin, which is restricted in some reference countries; Stripe is the bank-ending path.
