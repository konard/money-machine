## How it works

You build assets (models, materials, VFX, editor tools, code systems) and list them on Fab (Epic, 88% to seller), Unity Asset Store (70% standard share) and CGTrader (tiered 55-80% based on rolling 365-day sales). Buyers are indie devs and studios saving production time. Code tools/editor extensions often outsell raw models. Fab requires the 'Created with AI' flag on products made with generative AI.

**Platforms:** Fab (Epic Games), Unity Asset Store, CGTrader, Sketchfab

## Payout path

- **Methods:** Fab: third-party payment provider (bank/PayPal by region), Unity: PayPal monthly or bank transfer quarterly (USD only), CGTrader: regular payout method in USD
- **Minimum payout:** Fab $100 (rolls over; paid regardless after 12 months); Unity per provider agreement; CGTrader none stated for sales ($10 for subscription earnings)
- **Frequency:** monthly (Fab, Unity PayPal), weekly on Wednesdays (CGTrader)
- **Typical delay:** 30 days
- **Notes:** Fab pays ~30 days after month end; CGTrader pays sales 20 days after sale (first payout no earlier than 20 days after KYC). Refunds deducted from next payout; tax forms required; Unity requires 2FA for payout profile.
