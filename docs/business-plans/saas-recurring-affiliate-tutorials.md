## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$80 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $8,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **4/10**. Expect the first payment after 30–180 d.

## How it works

Create step-by-step tutorials (blog + YouTube + GitHub templates) that solve a concrete problem with a tool, e.g. 'deploy X on a VPS' or 'automate Y with an AI tool'. Viewers who sign up through your link generate commissions. Official 2026 terms: Kit (ConvertKit) pays 50% of a referral's first 12 months, plus 10% lifetime recurring once you refer 10 paying customers in a year; ElevenLabs pays up to 22% for 12 months; Semrush pays one-time $50-$300 per sale (up to $450 at higher tiers) plus $10 per trial with a 120-day cookie; Hostinger starts at 40% per sale. Notion's program (20% of first-year revenue) is currently not accepting new affiliates. Recurring revenue compounds as tutorials keep ranking.

**Platforms:** PartnerStack, Impact.com, Rewardful/FirstPromoter (vendor-hosted), YouTube, Blog/GitHub

## What can be automated

- AI drafts tutorial scripts and code samples; agent verifies the code runs
- Auto-generate blog post from video transcript
- Link management with UTM + per-program dashboards
- Monitor tool pricing changes and update content

## Unavoidable manual steps

- Recording or voicing videos (or AI voice with disclosure)
- Applying to programs
- Hands-on testing of each tool

## Legality & compliance

Legal everywhere; follow each program's rules.

- **Compliance requirements:** affiliate disclosure (FTC/CAP); YouTube paid-promotion/affiliate disclosure; no self-referral or cookie stuffing
- **Platform availability:** Most SaaS programs accept affiliates worldwide and pay via PayPal/Stripe; a few exclude sanctioned countries.

## Risks

- Programs cut rates or end recurring terms
- Tool shutdown or pricing changes
- YouTube discovery is uncertain
- Trust damage if you shill weak tools
