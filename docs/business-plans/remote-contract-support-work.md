## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$100–$600 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $2,500 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **7/10**. Expect the first payment after 21–60 d.

## How it works

Companies outsource chat, email, phone support and community moderation to vendors who contract remote workers. ModSquad (mods in 90+ countries) pays hourly for moderation and support projects; Arise contracts independent micro-businesses in the US, Canada, UK, India, the Philippines, South Africa and parts of the Caribbean and Latin America; LiveOps (US) pays for talk time; Working Solutions (US/Canada only) pays contractors for time worked in 15-minute increments. After a background check and short unpaid training, you book shifts in a portal and are paid every two weeks or monthly by direct deposit or PayPal. This is work, not a business, and automation is minimal, but it is one of the most dependable ways to be paid online if you can commit scheduled hours.
