## How it works

You design a 2-6 week live course or a short workshop on a professional skill (e.g. product management, applied AI, data engineering) and list it on Maven. Students (often reimbursed by employers) pay $200-$2,000 per seat. Maven keeps 10% as platform fee (Stripe fees on top) and may promote strong courses in its marketplace. You teach live over Zoom with assignments and peer discussion; recordings can be re-sold as later cohorts.

**Platforms:** Maven, Zoom, Stripe
