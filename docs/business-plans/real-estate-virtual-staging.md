## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$400 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $5,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **5/10**. Expect the first payment after 7–60 d.

## How it works

Agents pay per image to make empty or messy rooms sell. Generative inpainting and staging tools do most of the work; you fix perspective, lighting and artifacts and keep structural features truthful. Market to agents and real-estate photographers who batch orders weekly. Staged images must be disclosed as virtually staged in listings.

**Platforms:** Upwork, Fiverr, direct to agents/photographers

## Legality & compliance

Legal; taxable. Do not alter permanent features (views, damage, room size); many MLS and consumer-protection rules require disclosing virtual staging.

- **Compliance requirements:** tax reporting; platform ID check; platform AI/misrepresentation rules; label images 'virtually staged'; no concealment of defects
- **Platform availability:** Upwork/Fiverr pay out in nearly all reference countries via Payoneer/bank.

## Risks

- agents adopting self-serve AI tools
- price pressure (big vendors $30/image)
- misrepresentation complaints

## Evidence that people get paid
