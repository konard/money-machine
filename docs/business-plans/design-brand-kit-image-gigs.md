## How it works

Logo design and background removal are among the most crowded gig categories, and free tools (Canva AI, Adobe Express, remove.bg-style tools) let small sellers do it themselves. Sellers still win with niche positioning (e.g. Shopify/Amazon sellers, specific industries) and fast bulk photo editing for e-commerce. Typical failure: competing on price with hundreds of $5 gigs and never ranking.

### Pricing strategy

Launch with a cheap fixed-scope tier (3 concepts or 20 photos) to earn reviews, then sell full brand kits and monthly photo-batch plans at higher tiers.

### Path to profit

1. Month 0: create portfolio of 10 mock brand kits and before/after photo sets; publish 2-3 gigs (cost $20)
2. Month 1-2: first orders at $15-$40, expected $50-$150
3. Month 3-6: niche down (e.g. Etsy/Amazon product photos), raise prices, add rush delivery
4. Month 6-12: recurring photo batches from online sellers; target $500+/month if reviews accumulate

## Legality & compliance

Legal everywhere; taxable. Purely AI-generated images may not be copyrightable (e.g. US), so disclose and add human vector work for logos.

- **Compliance requirements:** tax reporting; platform ID check; Fiverr AI standards / Upwork no-misrepresentation; use tools whose licenses allow commercial output; no imitation of existing trademarks
- **Platform availability:** Fiverr/Upwork pay out almost everywhere via Payoneer; PayPal receiving missing in some (e.g. Bangladesh).

## Risks

- very crowded category
- copyright/trademark disputes over AI logos
- client revisions eat margin

## Evidence that people get paid

Fiverr runs dedicated logo and image-editing categories with high order volume; its AI guidelines allow AI if seller holds rights to delivered content.
