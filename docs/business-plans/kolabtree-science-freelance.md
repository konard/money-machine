## How it works

Clients (biotech, pharma, food, med-device startups, academics) post projects such as literature reviews, statistical analysis, medical writing or regulatory documents. Experts send proposals and work to a statement of work with funded milestones. A PhD/MSc researcher anywhere can compete on expertise, not location. AI speeds literature searches and drafts, but experts sign off on accuracy.

## Payout path

- **Methods:** Payoneer -> local bank
- **Minimum payout:** USD 49.99 balance before payout
- **Frequency:** per milestone release
- **Typical delay:** 7 days
- **Notes:** Payoneer is the only payout method; payout needs a USD 49.99 minimum balance. Kolabtree pays sending costs; the freelancer bears a 2% conversion charge plus any intermediary/receiving bank fees. The freelancer service fee is not stated on official pages; third-party profiles report up to 20%.

- apply and get approved as an expert

## Legality & compliance

Legal everywhere. Medical/regulatory writing needs domain competence; no ghost-writing that breaks academic-integrity rules.

- **Compliance requirements:** tax reporting; Payoneer KYC; confidentiality/NDA; no academic misconduct
- **Platform availability:** Payoneer does not serve Iraq (per Kolabtree help); all other reference countries are fine. Expert approval is selective (usually postgraduate).

## Risks

- selective approval
- sparse projects in some fields
- liability for wrong analysis
