## How it works

After acceptance (social account review) and onsite approval of test videos, you record honest 1-3 minute reviews of products you own and upload them to Amazon. Amazon places them on product detail pages; purchases attributed to your video earn onsite commissions, plus storefront/link commissions. Income comes from a growing video library without needing your own traffic. Payer is Amazon via the Associates payment system.

**Platforms:** Amazon Influencer Program, Amazon Associates

## Legality & compliance

Legal; videos must be genuine reviews of products you have used (no fake/incentivized reviews).

- **Compliance requirements:** Amazon Influencer/Associates policies; genuine first-hand review only; affiliate disclosure (FTC/CAP)
- **Platform availability:** Most mature in Amazon US; also UK, DE, CA, IN and other stores with smaller programs. Shoppers must be in the store's market.

## Risks

- Saturation on popular ASINs
- Product costs if not already owned
