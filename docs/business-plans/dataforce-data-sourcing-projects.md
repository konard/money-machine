Most people who try this earn around **$0–$40 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $400 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **5/10**. Expect the first payment after 21–120 d.

## How it works

DataForce posts country- and language-specific projects: voice recordings in Indian and other low-resource languages, first-person video of daily tasks, shopping-experience ratings and annotation. You apply per project, complete it, pass a quality check, then get paid per task, per hour or a flat fee. Speakers of under-served languages qualify for projects that English speakers cannot do.

## Legality & compliance

Legal everywhere. You license your own voice/likeness under the project consent; read the data-use terms before recording.

- **Compliance requirements:** W-8/W-9 tax form; informed consent for biometric data; tax reporting
- **Platform availability:** Contributor sign-up is open in almost every country, but each project targets specific locales; many are on-site (e.g. Pune, Hyderabad). Count only remote ones.
