## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$15 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $1,500 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **6/10**. Expect the first payment after 14–120 d.

## How it works

You record 30 min to 3 h of clean studio-quality speech and create a Professional Voice Clone (PVC), then share it in the public Voice Library with a description, tags and a notice period. Paying ElevenLabs users pick your voice for narration, ads, games and agents; ElevenLabs pays you per character generated by paid users. The work is front-loaded: after verification the voice earns with no further recording. Distinct, well-described niche voices (accents, ages, styles) earn more than generic ones.

Licensing your own voice is legal everywhere in the reference set; you may only clone your OWN voice (identity verification enforces this).

- **Compliance requirements:** report income for tax; Stripe KYC; voice ownership verification; US tax withholding on US-customer usage
- **Platform availability:** Depends on Stripe Connect payout support for your country; unsupported countries cannot receive payouts.
