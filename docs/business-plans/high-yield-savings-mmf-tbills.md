## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$3–$30 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $400 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **10/10**. Expect the first payment after 30–60 d.

## How it works

You move cash from a zero-interest account into an insured high-yield savings account, a government money-market fund, or buy short-dated government bills directly (TreasuryDirect, Tesouro Direto, NS&I, Singapore T-bills, Nigerian/Kenyan T-bill auctions). The bank or government pays interest because it is borrowing your money. Returns scale with capital; a baseline, not a business.

**Platforms:** Online banks (Marcus, Ally, Chase UK, Trade Republic), TreasuryDirect, Tesouro Direto, Vanguard/Fidelity MMFs, local T-bill auctions
