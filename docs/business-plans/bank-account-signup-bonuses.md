## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$150 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $600 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **8/10**. Expect the first payment after 45–150 d.

## How it works

Offers are country-specific and change monthly. Examples checked Nov 2026: in the US, Chase pays $400 for a new Total Checking account with $1,000+ of direct deposits within 90 days (offer ends 27 Jan 2027, paid within 15 days); in the UK, CASS switch bonuses range GBP 175-300 (Barclays up to GBP 300, Santander GBP 240, HSBC GBP 220, First Direct GBP 210, Lloyds/NatWest GBP 200). Canada and Germany run similar but smaller offers. You open the account, route a payroll or qualifying deposit, meet card/debit conditions, receive the bonus, then keep or close the account after the clawback window. Banks pay because customer lifetime value exceeds the bonus.

**Platforms:** Chase/Wells Fargo/SoFi (US), UK CASS switching offers, Canadian big-5 banks, German direct banks, Doctor of Credit / MoneySavingExpert trackers

## Payout path

- **Methods:** bonus credited to the new bank account
- **Minimum payout:** Fixed bonus per offer (e.g. US $400 Chase; UK GBP 175-300)
- **Frequency:** one-off per offer
- **Typical delay:** 90 days
- **Notes:** Chase pays within 15 days of meeting requirements; UK switch bonuses typically within weeks of the switch. Many banks claw back if the account is closed within ~6 months. Requires an existing salary/pay-in of roughly $1,000-$2,000 to route (not spent), hence capitalRequiredUsd 0.

## Legality & compliance

Legal everywhere (standard retail promotion); bonuses are usually taxable interest income.

- **Compliance requirements:** KYC with real identity; meet direct-deposit terms honestly; report bonus income for tax; avoid excessive ChexSystems/credit inquiries
- **Platform availability:** Country-specific: meaningful recurring offers exist mainly in the US, UK, Canada, Germany and Australia (~5-8 of 50 reference countries). Residency, a local address and often a salary paid in are required. Examples above are dated Nov 2026 and expire; always check the bank's own offer page.
