## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$40 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $3,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **5/10**. Expect the first payment after 45–180 d.

## How it works

You upload a finished, human-authored ebook once to an aggregator that ships it to Apple Books, Kobo (incl. Kobo Plus), Barnes & Noble, Everand, Tolino and library platforms (OverDrive, Hoopla, cloudLibrary, BorrowBox). Readers and libraries pay the stores; the aggregator forwards royalties monthly. Draft2Digital keeps about 10% of the retail price and charges new accounts a one-time $20 activation fee; PublishDrive charges a subscription instead and passes 100% of royalties (one ebook to Apple, B&N and Kobo is free). Libraries are a distinct payer (licences or pay-per-checkout).

Going wide pays best for authors with series and a mailing list who run price promotions across Apple, Kobo and libraries. Non-Amazon stores are smaller and slower to build, and AI-generated books crowd every store. A single standalone book rarely clears $100/month wide. Calibration: profit likelihood lowered from 15% to 10% because going wide forgoes Amazon (the dominant ebook store) and KU, so it should not beat the KDP entry (12%).

### Pricing strategy

Make book 1 of a series permafree or $0.99 and price later books at $4.99-$6.99; set library prices at 2-3x retail.

## Legality & compliance

Writing and selling original text is legal in all reference countries; royalties are taxable.

- **Compliance requirements:** tax interview / W-8BEN; D2D rejects fully AI-generated content and AI nonfiction from non-credentialled authors
- **Platform availability:** Payoneer and direct deposit cover all 50 reference countries; store reach varies by country.
