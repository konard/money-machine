## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$30 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $3,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **5/10**. Expect the first payment after 30–180 d.

## How it works

You write or compile books (nonfiction guides, fiction, workbooks, puzzle/activity books) and upload to KDP; Amazon prints on demand and pays 35-70% ebook royalties, ~60% minus print cost on paperbacks, plus Kindle Unlimited page reads. Amazon requires you to disclose AI-generated text, images or translations (AI-assisted editing need not be disclosed) and caps new titles at 3 per day per account. Low-content books have little moat, so niche research and quality covers matter.

**Platforms:** Amazon KDP, Kindle Unlimited

## Payout path

- **Methods:** EFT direct deposit, wire, check
- **Minimum payout:** $0 for EFT (usually no minimum); $100 for wire/check on Amazon.com
- **Frequency:** monthly

- **Notes:** Royalties paid ~60 days after the end of the month of sale (90 days for Expanded Distribution). AI disclosure rule is on KDP's content guidelines; the 3-titles/day cap was confirmed by KDP to the Authors Guild (2023) but is not on a KDP help page.
