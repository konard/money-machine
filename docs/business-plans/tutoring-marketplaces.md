## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$100–$600 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $4,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **7/10**. Expect the first payment after 7–45 d.

## How it works

Create a tutor profile with intro video and set an hourly rate. Students find you via search, book a trial, then buy packages. Preply keeps the trial and 18-33% after; italki ~15%; Wyzant (US) 25%. Strongest demand: English, native languages, exam prep, math.

**Platforms:** Preply, italki, Wyzant

## What can be automated

- AI-generated lesson plans, homework and level tests
- Auto-scheduled reminders and post-lesson summaries
- AI feedback on student writing before lessons

## Unavoidable manual steps

- Teaching live lessons (fully manual)
- Profile video
- KYC and payout setup

## Legality & compliance

Legal everywhere; contractor income. Wyzant tutors must be US residents.

- **Compliance requirements:** identity verification; tax reporting
- **Platform availability:** Preply and italki accept tutors from most countries and pay via Payoneer/PayPal/Wise; Wyzant is US-only.

## Risks

- Low rates in competitive languages
- Time-for-money, no scaling
