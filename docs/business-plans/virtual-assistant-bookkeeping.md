## Legality & compliance

VA and bookkeeping are legal; tax filing/advice and statutory accounting need licenses in many countries (e.g. Germany StBerG), so limit to bookkeeping/data entry.

- **Compliance requirements:** no tax advice without license; confidentiality; contractor tax reporting
- **Platform availability:** BELAY accepts US residents except CA, CO, MT, NJ, NY, PA. Fancy Hands' application page states no residency rule (historically US-focused). OnlineJobs.ph targets Filipino workers. Upwork covers most reference countries.
