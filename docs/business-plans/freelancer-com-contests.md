## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$80 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $1,500 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **4/10**. Expect the first payment after 7–120 d.

## How it works

Contest posters pre-fund the prize; entering is free and the winner gets the prize minus a fee. AI tools can make many concept entries quickly, then you refine the shortlisted ones. Winning builds reviews that help project bids. Bidding needs a USD 20 balance kept in your account, which is why this entry lists USD 20 capital.

### Pricing strategy

No pricing control in contests; use contest wins as reviews to move into fixed-price bids priced 10-20% below the median, then raise rates.
