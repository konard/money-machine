## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$2–$5 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $20 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **8/10**. Expect the first payment after 30–90 d.

## How it works

Audience-measurement firms recruit panelists who install a meter on phones or computers. Nielsen pays up to about $50–$60 a year per device in points plus prize draws; MobileXpression gives weekly credits; SavvyConnect (SurveySavvy) pays $3 per device type per month. Rewards are redeemed for PayPal cash or gift cards. It is fully passive after installation, the sums are small and certain, and the trade-off is privacy: everything the device does is recorded.

**Platforms:** Nielsen Computer & Mobile Panel, MobileXpression, SavvyConnect

## Legality & compliance

Legal: you consent to measurement of your own devices. Do not install on employer devices or devices used by others without consent.

- **Compliance requirements:** own device only; age 18+; one panel membership per person
- **Platform availability:** Nielsen panels run in the US, UK, Germany, Australia and a few others; MobileXpression and SavvyConnect are US-only.
