## Realistic expectations

## How it works

You design a reusable Notion workspace template and publish it to the Notion Marketplace (direct paid sales via Stripe after joining a waitlist and being approved, which can take months) or sell the duplicate link through Gumroad/Lemon Squeezy (also allowed as an external link from a Marketplace listing). Buyers are Notion users seeking ready-made systems. Notion charges 8% + $0.40 per transaction, handles sales tax/VAT and pays biweekly. Discovery inside the Marketplace plus a free-template funnel are the main traffic sources.

**Platforms:** Notion Marketplace, Gumroad
