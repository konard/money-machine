## How it works

You produce tracks (ambient, lo-fi, focus, library-style music) and release via a distributor that delivers to DSPs and collects royalties. Spotify only pays on tracks with 1,000+ streams/12 months, filters spam uploads and bans unauthorized voice clones; Deezer tags fully AI tracks and excludes them from recommendations. DistroKid accepts AI-assisted music if you own the rights and it is not mass-generated spam. Real listening (playlists, sync, content creators) is what pays.

**Platforms:** DistroKid, TuneCore, Spotify, Apple Music
