## How it works

GTA V/RDR2 roleplay servers on Rockstar's Cfx.re platform buy jobs, inventories, housing, maps (MLOs) and UIs. Creators sell from a Tebex store; approved Tebex Partners can also list items on the curated Cfx Marketplace at no extra commission. Rockstar's Creator Platform License lets Rockstar and the operator act as agents licensing content to server admins. The licence forbids cash-out mechanics, loot boxes, real-money currency, crypto/NFTs and reselling third-party content.
