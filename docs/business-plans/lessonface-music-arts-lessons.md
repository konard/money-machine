## How it works

Lessonface is a public-benefit marketplace for live online lessons, mostly music (voice, guitar, violin, piano), plus art, dance and language. You set your own rates and schedule. Students found by Lessonface cost 15%, students you refer cost 5%, and group classes, self-paced courses and downloadables cost up to 30%. Its booking, payments and video room make it a full back office for your own students, while the marketplace adds new ones.

## Legality & compliance

Teaching is legal everywhere; a background check applies.

- **Compliance requirements:** teaching experience (2+ years) or 5 years professional experience; interview and background check; report income for tax
- **Platform availability:** Payouts run through PayPal, so they reach most reference countries where PayPal can receive money (not e.g. Bangladesh, Iraq, Libya or Tunisia). Lessonface lists teachers outside the US, but it has no official list of countries it accepts teachers from.

## Risks

- Small marketplace with limited inbound demand
- Payout only via PayPal for new teachers (PayPal fees, no PayPal receiving in some countries)
- Music-teaching niche needs real skill
