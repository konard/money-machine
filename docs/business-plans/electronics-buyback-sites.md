## How it works

Buyback services quote a price by model and condition, send a prepaid shipping label, inspect the device and pay by PayPal, direct deposit or cheque. Decluttr (US/UK) also buys games, DVDs and books by scanning barcodes. Swappa and Back Market are marketplaces with buyer protection where you set the price and typically earn 20–40% more than buyback quotes. It is a one-off, not recurring, but the probability of being paid is close to certain and most households have unused devices.
