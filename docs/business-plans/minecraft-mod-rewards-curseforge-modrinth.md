## How it works

Publish free mods and modpacks. CurseForge hands out points from a monthly pool by project popularity once a project passes a threshold; each point is worth $0.05. Modpack authors also get 65% of a user's first month on BisectHosting servers bought through their page. Modrinth pays creators 75% of its ad revenue, split by views and downloads. Mojang's EULA bars selling mods, so ad-share is the sanctioned route.

## Payout path

- **Notes:** CurseForge PayPal/Payoneer orders are processed in the first days of each month (terms allow up to 60 days after redemption), and points expire 3 years after accrual. Modrinth revenue is NET 60: it becomes available 60 days after the last day of the month in which it was earned.
