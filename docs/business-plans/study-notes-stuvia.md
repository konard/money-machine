## How it works

Students and professionals upload original study notes, summaries and exam-prep guides. Buyers searching for a course or exam pay per document; Stuvia takes a per-sale commission (shown when you set the price; reviews put it near 30%) and lets you withdraw once your wallet reaches EUR/USD/GBP 10. Docsity uses a points system convertible to cash. Only sell notes you wrote; reproducing textbooks, professors' slides or exam papers violates copyright and university rules.
