## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$500 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $8,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **4/10**. Expect the first payment after 7–90 d.

## How it works

Founders pay to look credible to investors. You sell fixed packages: deck rewrite + redesign (10-20 slides), and a driver-based financial model in Google Sheets/Excel. LLMs draft the narrative, market-size math and slide copy; templates and AI image tools speed design; a model template covers most SaaS/marketplace cases. You review numbers with the founder; projections are the founder's assumptions, not advice.

**Platforms:** Upwork, Fiverr (incl. Fiverr Pro), Contra, direct

- **Frequency:** on demand after clearance

- **Notes:** Upwork: variable 0-15% freelancer service fee set per contract since May 2025 (pre-May-2025 contracts 10%), per Upwork's SEC 10-Q; Upwork help pages block automated fetch.
