## How it works

Clients send audio of their own songs or performances and you deliver notation (MuseScore/Sibelius/Dorico files, PDF, Guitar Pro tabs) or MIDI. AI audio-to-notation tools produce a first draft; you correct rhythm, voicing and layout. Buyers include songwriters registering works, teachers, worship bands and players who need lead sheets.

**Platforms:** Fiverr, Upwork, SoundBetter

## Legality & compliance

Legal for the client's own or public-domain works. Selling transcriptions of copyrighted songs to third parties requires a license; for client private use, rules vary by country.

- **Compliance requirements:** transcribe only works the client owns/controls or public domain, or for private study; tax reporting; platform ID verification
- **Platform availability:** Fiverr/Upwork pay out almost everywhere via Payoneer. PayPal receiving is limited in a few reference countries (e.g. Bangladesh, Libya, Iraq); Payoneer/Wise or local alternatives may be needed.
