## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$30 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $3,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **3/10**. Expect the first payment after 30–240 d.

## How it works

Build a niche deal site (e.g. developer tools, outdoor gear, a specific country's e-commerce) that verifies codes and curates time-limited deals, ideally with a deals newsletter and alerts. Clicks go through merchant affiliate links or a sub-network (Skimlinks, Sovrn Commerce) which auto-affiliates thousands of merchants. You only list codes merchants actually released; paying publishers are merchants via networks. Generic coupon SEO is heavily contested and Google penalises third-party coupon sections on unrelated sites.

**Platforms:** Skimlinks, Sovrn Commerce, Awin/CJ/Impact merchant programs, Email newsletter

## Legality & compliance

Legal; never publish fake or leaked codes (consumer-protection law). UK ASA/CAP guidance specifically requires voucher sites to label affiliate offers (e.g. 'Ad' in or around the title).

- **Compliance requirements:** affiliate disclosure (FTC/CAP); merchant coupon policies (only authorised codes); consumer-protection rules on price claims
- **Platform availability:** Skimlinks/Sovrn accept publishers globally; merchants mostly US/UK/EU.

## Risks

- Google site-reputation/coupon spam enforcement
- Merchants cancel commissions for coupon sites
- Last-click attribution disputes
- Thin margins

## Evidence that people get paid
