## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$50 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $5,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **3/10**. Expect the first payment after 30–180 d.

## How it works

Creator affiliate platforms aggregate retailer programs: you apply, then build storefronts/collections (outfits, home, beauty, gear) and share links on Instagram, blog, YouTube or newsletter. When followers buy, the platform collects commission from retailers and pays you your share; brands also offer gifting and paid collabs inside the platform. Works best with an existing lifestyle audience.

**Platforms:** LTK, ShopMy

## Legality & compliance

Legal; disclosure needed on every post.

- **Compliance requirements:** affiliate disclosure (FTC/CAP); platform creator terms; tax forms
- **Platform availability:** Strongest for US/UK/EU/AU audiences; retailers mainly ship to those countries; creators elsewhere can join but conversions are low.

## Risks

- Approval favours established creators
- Long lock periods
- Retailer rate changes
- Audience dependence

## Evidence that people get paid

ShopMy's official payment FAQ documents the $11 minimum, weekly Friday payouts and 30-120 day pending period; LTK pays thousands of creators but its payout terms could not be re-verified.
