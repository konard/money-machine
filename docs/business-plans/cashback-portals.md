## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$2–$15 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $150 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **8/10**. Expect the first payment after 45–120 d.

## How it works

Cashback sites (Rakuten, TopCashback, Quidco, ShopBack, iGraal, Cashrewards) earn affiliate commissions from merchants and pass most of it to you. You install the browser extension, shop through it, the merchant confirms the order after the return window, and the portal pays you by PayPal or bank. Earnings depend on your own spending (typically 1-10% back) plus referral bonuses for inviting friends. Not a business, but a near-automatic rebate.

**Platforms:** Rakuten, TopCashback, Quidco, ShopBack, iGraal, Cashrewards
