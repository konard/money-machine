## How it works

You arrange songs for specific instruments/levels (easy piano, string quartet, SATB) in notation software and upload PDFs or interactive scores to ArrangeMe, which clears copyrighted songs from its approved list and distributes to Sheet Music Plus, Sheet Music Direct, MuseScore and partner retailers. Teachers, students and ensembles buy them. Musicnotes Marketplace is a parallel channel. AI transcription tools speed up drafts but humans must check notation.

**Platforms:** ArrangeMe, Sheet Music Plus, Sheet Music Direct, Musicnotes Marketplace

## Legality & compliance

Legal: ArrangeMe obtains licenses for approved copyrighted songs; arranging unlicensed copyrighted songs outside such programs infringes copyright.

- **Compliance requirements:** only arrange songs on the approved list or public domain/originals; W-9/W-8BEN; accurate credits
- **Platform availability:** ArrangeMe accepts sellers internationally; PayPal payout. PayPal receiving is limited in a few reference countries (e.g. Bangladesh, Libya, Iraq); Payoneer/Wise or local alternatives may be needed.

## Risks

- Low 10% commission on copyrighted titles
- Exclusivity rules on copyrighted arrangements
- Competition from free scores
