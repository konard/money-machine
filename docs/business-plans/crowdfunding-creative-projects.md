## How it works

You build a campaign page with a video, reward tiers (digital copies, early access, credits) and a funding goal. Backers pledge; on Kickstarter you only collect if the goal is met (5% platform fee + 3-5% processing). Indiegogo allows flexible funding. Digital rewards avoid shipping costs.

**Platforms:** Kickstarter, Indiegogo

## Legality & compliance

Reward-based crowdfunding is legal; equity crowdfunding excluded. Must deliver promised rewards.

- **Compliance requirements:** deliver rewards; consumer protection; tax reporting
- **Platform availability:** Kickstarter creators must reside in one of 25 countries (AU, AT, BE, CA, DK, FR, DE, GR, HK, IE, IT, JP, LU, MX, NL, NZ, NO, PL, SG, SI, ES, SE, CH, UK, US) with local ID and bank account: only 10 of the ~50 reference countries. Indiegogo accepts more countries (not re-verified).
