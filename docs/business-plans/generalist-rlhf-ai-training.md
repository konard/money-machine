## How it works

AI labs pay data vendors for human preference data. After you pass a writing and reasoning assessment, projects appear in your dashboard, and you are paid hourly or per task for rating, rewriting or red-teaming LLM output. The work is irregular, and projects can disappear without notice. Vendors usually forbid using AI to produce your answers, so this is human labour. Scale wound Remotasks into Outlier.

**Platforms:** DataAnnotation, Outlier (Scale AI), Remotasks (legacy)

## Legality & compliance

Legal everywhere. These are independent-contractor arrangements, and worker classification is being litigated (reported 2025 class action against DataAnnotation).

- **Compliance requirements:** report income for tax; ID verification; no AI-generated submissions where forbidden
- **Platform availability:** Each project limits which countries it accepts. Outlier cites 50 countries and requires a valid ID and phone from your country of residence. DataAnnotation requires English fluency; its country list is not published (reported as US/UK/CA/AU/NZ/IE). Remotasks cut off several African and South Asian countries in 2024.
