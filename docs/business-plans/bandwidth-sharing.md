## How it works

Proxy companies route customers' traffic (price monitoring, ad verification, scraping) through your home IP. You install the app and leave it running. A typical income is about $1-3 per device per month, so reaching Honeygain's $20 minimum can take many months; Pawns pays from $5. You are legally exposed because unknown third parties' traffic leaves your IP, and Honeygain's terms make you responsible for checking that your ISP allows it.

## Legality & compliance

Legal in itself, but it often breaches residential ISP terms; Honeygain's terms require you to check your ISP contract and accept risks such as blocklisting. Avoid it on employer, shared or metered networks. Honeygain excludes sanctioned regions (incl. occupied Ukrainian regions), not whole reference countries.

- **Compliance requirements:** check ISP terms; do not run via VPN/proxy; report income for tax
- **Platform availability:** Available in most countries. Earnings are much lower in low-demand geographies.
