## How it works

AI labs and Gray Swan run sanctioned challenges where breaking the model's safeguards is the goal. Gray Swan Arena runs free, time-bound prize challenges (e.g. a $300K indirect prompt-injection wave in Nov 2026) and splits each pool across the leaderboard; Anthropic runs an ongoing, application/invite-only model safety bounty on HackerOne paying up to $35K per novel universal jailbreak. OpenAI's Bugcrowd bounty pays for product security bugs only; jailbreaks and model-behavior issues are explicitly out of scope. AI agents can help generate and test attack variants within each program's rules. Only participate in authorized scopes; never publish or misuse harmful outputs.
