Most people who try this earn around **$20–$150 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $1,500 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **7/10**. Expect the first payment after 14–60 d.

## How it works

Gengo and similar marketplaces split client documents into small jobs that qualified translators claim from a dashboard. Pay is per word (Gengo's standard tier is a few cents per word) and payouts go to PayPal or Payoneer on a fixed schedule. Unbabel pays for post-editing machine translation per task. AI can pre-translate, but the platforms require human quality; using raw machine output is detectable and leads to removal. For bilingual people this is one of the most reliable small online incomes.

## Payout path

- **Methods:** PayPal, Payoneer
- **Minimum payout:** No stated minimum (PayPal fee 1.5%, min $1.50)
- **Frequency:** on request; processed on the 5th and 20th (Gengo)
- **Typical delay:** 15 days
- **Notes:** Gengo help centre: payouts are not automatic. Request at least 7 days ahead; paid on the 5th and 20th in USD via PayPal (1.5% fee, min $1.50, max $20) or Payoneer (no Gengo fee). A tax form is required before the first payout. Rates differ by language pair and are shown on each job. Unbabel's editor programme page still exists but its pay terms were not confirmed.
