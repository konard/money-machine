## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$30 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $5,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **4/10**. Expect the first payment after 14–120 d.

## How it works

Make mesh fashion, furniture or scripted items in Blender/LSL and sell them in-world or on the SL Marketplace for Linden Dollars. Sell the L$ on the LindeX for a USD balance, then withdraw it with a Process Credit request through Tilia/Thunes. The economy is mature and buyers spend steadily, but sales are concentrated among top brands.

**Platforms:** Second Life, SL Marketplace, LindeX, Tilia (Thunes)

## Payout path

- **Methods:** Process Credit via Tilia -> PayPal or other payout account (depends on location)
- **Minimum payout:** $3 plus fees per withdrawal (max $9,999 minus fees)
- **Frequency:** on request
- **Typical delay:** 10 days
- **Notes:** Most requests take 5-10 business days, some up to 30. A per-transaction fee varies by method and location. Linden Lab set the LindeX sell fee at 5% from March 2023 (official blog bot-blocked; confirmed via ModemWorld's report of the announcement). The Marketplace commission (10% since 2020) was not re-verified.

## Legality & compliance

Selling virtual goods you made is legal and allowed by Linden Lab. Avoid SL's gambling-related products and IP knock-offs; keep products in General/Moderate maturity for this catalog.

- **Compliance requirements:** Second Life ToS; Tilia identity verification and billing info; tax reporting
- **Platform availability:** Withdrawal methods depend on location; Tilia country coverage not verified. Small L$ upload fees (about L$10 per mesh upload) count as near-zero capital.

## Risks

- Ageing user base and strong competition
- Fees and limits on L$ conversion
- Content theft (copybot)

Second Life's official knowledge base (fetched 2026-11) documents Process Credit through Tilia: $3 minimum, $9,999 maximum and 5-10 business days.
