## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$30 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $1,500 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **4/10**. Expect the first payment after 30–240 d.

## How it works

Unlike own-shop POD, you never touch orders or costs: Redbubble, TeePublic and Amazon Merch on Demand list your design on products in their own storefronts. You earn a margin/royalty per sale (Redbubble: artist margin minus tier-based platform/excess-markup fees, capped at $150 per period, Pro tier exempt; Amazon Merch: third-party reports say royalties moved to traffic-based tiers on June 1, 2026, with the default tier paying ~50% of the old royalty). Amazon Merch requires application approval and has upload tiers.

## Payout path

- **Methods:** Redbubble: PayPal or bank transfer, TeePublic: PayPal or Payoneer only (Stripe used for identity verification), Amazon Merch: bank deposit
- **Minimum payout:** Redbubble $10/£10/€10 (lowered from $20 on July 1, 2026); TeePublic: no minimum stated in help center (Payoneer minimum unverified); Amazon Merch: monthly deposit
- **Frequency:** monthly
- **Typical delay:** 45 days
- **Notes:** Redbubble processes at the start of each month for orders shipped the previous month, landing by the 15th; TeePublic pays on the 15th for the previous calendar month's sales; Amazon Merch timing (~30 days after month end) not verified from an official page (Merch resources are login-only).
