## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$2 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $200 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **2/10**. Expect the first payment after 60–365 d.

## How it works

On Reddit, redditors 18+ in supported countries with 100+ karma (5,000+ for Top Contributor) earn from Gold that other users spend on awards for their content (reported $0.009-$0.01 per Gold, $10 minimum; unused Gold cannot be cashed out). On Quora, Space owners can earn ad revenue share and Quora+ subscription revenue proportional to member reading. Both reward genuinely helpful, human contributions; automated or AI-spam posting violates rules and gets accounts banned, so automation is limited to research.
