## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$20 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $5,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **4/10**. Expect the first payment after 30–180 d.

## How it works

Twitch Affiliate unlocks after 50 followers, 500 minutes on 7 days and 3 average viewers in 30 days; you earn ~50-70% of subs, Bits and ad share. Kick unlocks subscriptions after 5 cumulative streamed hours with a 95/5 split; its Partner Program (75 avg viewers, 30 h, 25 subs, 250 chatters in 30 days, or Fast Track) pays per stream. Viewers and advertisers pay. Live streaming is mostly manual; AI helps with overlays, clips, moderation and VOD repurposing. Gambling streams are excluded here (rule 3).
