## How it works

Companies and public bodies pay as accessibility laws tighten (US ADA Title II rule; EU Accessibility Act covers e-commerce). Scanners find part of the issues; you add keyboard/screen-reader testing, write the report with LLM help, and fix code or tag PDFs. Monitoring retainers follow.

## Legality & compliance

Legal; taxable. Audits are opinions, not legal advice; avoid 'guaranteed compliant' or certification claims.

- **Compliance requirements:** tax reporting; no legal-advice or certification claims; authorized testing on client properties only; professional indemnity insurance advisable
