## How it works

Trial lawyers test arguments on mock juries before court. Sites such as OnlineVerdict and eJury email case summaries to registered adults in the relevant county or federal district; you read the materials (20–60 minutes), answer questions and are paid per case. OnlineVerdict also runs Zoom mock trials paying $75–$700. Cases are infrequent (a few per year unless you live in a large metro), so the income is tiny, but the payout is near-certain and the work is interesting.
