## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$50 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $5,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **2/10**. Expect the first payment after 30–365 d.

## How it works

Run an own-domain site on Ghost or WordPress + Memberful where premium posts, archives, templates or videos require a paid Stripe membership. You own the domain and email list. Ghost takes 0% of revenue (Ghost(Pro) Publisher $29/mo billed yearly, or self-host on a cheap VPS); Memberful Standard charges $49/mo plus 4.9%.

**Platforms:** Ghost, Memberful, Stripe
