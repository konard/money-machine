## How it works

Choice of Games commissions interactive novels: you pitch, get an outline accepted, and receive a $10,000 advance against 25% royalties, or $15,000 against 10%, paid in milestones that start with $100 for an invited pitch. On the Hosted Games route you write a game of at least 30,000 words, run a public beta for a month, and receive 25% of revenue from app, Steam and web sales. Players pay for the game, and in-app purchases add revenue. AI-generated prose, code and art are banned.

**Platforms:** Choice of Games, Hosted Games
