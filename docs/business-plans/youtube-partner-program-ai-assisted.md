## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$30 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $5,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **3/10**. Expect the first payment after 90–540 d.

## How it works

You run a niche channel (explainers, tutorials, data stories) where AI helps with research, scripts, voiceover and editing but each video adds original value. After 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 qualified long-form watch hours in 365 days (or 10M Shorts views in 90 days) you apply to YPP. Advertisers pay Google; creators get 55% of long-form ad revenue and 45% of the Shorts pool share. Since July 2025 the 'inauthentic content' policy demonetizes mass-produced, template or repetitive videos, so volume farming fails.

**Platforms:** YouTube, Google AdSense

### Pricing strategy

Ad rates are set by advertisers; pick high-CPM topics (software, finance, B2B tools) and 8+ minute videos for mid-rolls. Once past 10k subscribers, add sponsor slots priced at ~$15-30 per 1,000 expected views and affiliate links.
