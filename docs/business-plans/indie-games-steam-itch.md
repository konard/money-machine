## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$30 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $5,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **3/10**. Expect the first payment after 60–540 d.

## How it works

Build a small, polished game or tool (or asset packs) and sell to players. itch.io lets you set your own revenue share and pays via PayPal/Payoneer; Steam requires a $100 Steam Direct fee (recouped after $1,000 adjusted gross) and takes 30%, but offers vastly more traffic. Payers are gamers. Results are hit-driven; most games earn under $1,000 lifetime.

**Platforms:** Steam, itch.io
