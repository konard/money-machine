## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$5–$15 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $40 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **7/10**. Expect the first payment after 14–60 d.

## How it works

Slicethepie plays you a track clip and you write a short critique and rating; better-quality reviews raise your star rating and per-review pay. Labels and brands use aggregated listener feedback for market research. Pay per review is small but available with no audience or skills.
