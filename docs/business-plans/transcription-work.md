## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$20–$150 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $800 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **6/10**. Expect the first payment after 7–60 d.

## How it works

Clients upload audio, and platforms split it into jobs that freelancers claim and transcribe or correct. Rev pays about $0.40-1.10 per audio minute, and TranscribeMe pays $15-22 per audio hour (average about $250/month). Most jobs now start from an ASR draft, which lowers pay per minute. Speed and accuracy decide your income. Entrance tests and waitlists are common.
