## How it works

Artists pay platforms like SubmitHub and Groover to send tracks to curators. Approved curators must listen and respond with feedback within a deadline; they are paid per reviewed premium submission whether or not they accept the song. Payment is for listening/feedback, not guaranteed placement, which keeps it within platform rules and anti-payola norms. Requires first building a real outlet with an audience to be accepted.

**Platforms:** SubmitHub, Groover

## Legality & compliance

Legal; paid review is permitted when no placement is guaranteed. Placements in exchange for money would breach platform rules and DSP playlist policies.

- **Compliance requirements:** never sell guaranteed placements; honest feedback; disclose sponsored content where required; tax reporting
- **Platform availability:** Both platforms accept curators internationally by application. Groover pays by Wise (some territories, e.g. Brazil and India, may be restricted) or PayPal; SubmitHub pays by PayPal only. PayPal receiving is limited in a few reference countries (e.g. Bangladesh, Libya, Iraq).
