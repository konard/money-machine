## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$500 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $10,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **4/10**. Expect the first payment after 14–90 d.

## How it works

Local and e-commerce SMBs pay a monthly management fee (often flat or % of spend) for campaign setup, tracking, creatives and optimization. Ad spend is billed to the client's own card in their own ad account; you get manager-account access. AI generates ad copy/creatives and reports; scripts flag waste. Google Partner status needs $10k 90-day managed spend, so start without it.

**Platforms:** Upwork, Fiverr, direct, Google Ads manager account, Meta Business Manager

## Payout path

- **Methods:** Stripe invoice -> bank, Wise, Payoneer, PayPal -> bank
- **Minimum payout:** none (direct invoices)
- **Frequency:** per milestone or monthly retainer
- **Typical delay:** 14 days
- **Notes:** Net-7 to net-30 invoices; take 30-50% deposits. Stripe missing in many reference countries; Wise/Payoneer cover most. Never front ad spend yourself.

## Legality & compliance

Legal; taxable. Advertising law (truthful ads, special categories like health/finance/housing) and platform ad policies apply.

- **Compliance requirements:** tax reporting; client owns ad accounts and pays spend; Google/Meta ad policies; conversion tracking consent (cookie banners in EU/UK); no guaranteed results claims
- **Platform availability:** Direct invoicing works almost everywhere; Google/Meta ads available in nearly all reference countries.
