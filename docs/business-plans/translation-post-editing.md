## How it works

Businesses localizing content pay for fluent human review of machine output. You run MT/LLM, then edit for accuracy, tone and terminology. Work comes via Smartcat marketplace, Upwork, Fiverr and agencies. Native speakers of mid-demand languages have an edge.

**Platforms:** Smartcat, Upwork, Fiverr, ProZ.com

## What can be automated

## Unavoidable manual steps

- human post-editing of every segment
- language tests/vetting
- client terminology queries

## Legality & compliance

Ordinary translation is legal everywhere. Certified/sworn/court translation requires a license in many countries; exclude it.

- **Compliance requirements:** no certified translations without license; disclose MT use when client requires; tax reporting
- **Platform availability:** Smartcat, Upwork and Fiverr pay out globally via Payoneer/PayPal/bank; demand depends on language pair.
