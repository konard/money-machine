## How it works

Sponsors post a dataset and metric with a prize pool. You build models, submit predictions, and top finishers are paid after code review and eligibility checks, usually with tax forms. AI coding agents can automate much of the feature engineering and model search. Prizes go to a handful of people per contest, so for most entrants the realistic gain is a portfolio.
