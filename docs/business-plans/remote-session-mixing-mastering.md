## How it works

You list productized services (e.g. 'mix up to 24 stems', 'session guitar track', 'female topline vocal', 'mastering per song') on AirGigs, SoundBetter and Fiverr's music categories. Clients upload stems or references, you deliver files, and the marketplace releases payment on completion. AI-assisted tools (stem separation, assistive mixing/mastering) cut turnaround, but the core value is human ears and performance.

**Platforms:** AirGigs, SoundBetter, Fiverr (Music & Audio, Fiverr Pro)

### Pricing strategy

Start with a low-priced 'first mix' gig ($30-50) with 2 revisions to collect reviews, then tier by stem count and raise to $100+ per mix after 15-20 reviews.

### Path to profit

1. Month 0: demo reel and 3 gig listings (cost $20)

3. Month 3-6: repeat clients and Fiverr level-up; $300-800/month

## Payout path

- **Methods:** PayPal -> bank, Payoneer -> bank
- **Minimum payout:** AirGigs: no stated minimum; Fiverr $1 PayPal / $20 Payoneer bank
- **Frequency:** on demand after clearance
- **Typical delay:** 10 days
- **Notes:** AirGigs: one-time $20 activation fee, 10-15% commission (min $8/order), 3-day clearing then daily PayPal withdrawals. Fiverr: 20% fee, 14-day clearance. SoundBetter (terms of use): clients fund jobs up front and funds are released when the client closes the job; the payment provider deducts a 'Platform Fee' that is shown in the proposal but not published as a percentage; Storefront sales carry a 10% commission plus a $9.99/month Storefront subscription; payouts run through a third-party processor (Stripe Connect named).

## Legality & compliance

Legal everywhere; taxable service income. Work-for-hire / credit terms should be agreed in writing.

- **Compliance requirements:** platform ID verification; tax reporting; written rights/credit terms; no use of client stems for other purposes
- **Platform availability:** AirGigs pays via PayPal (Payoneer beta); Fiverr pays via Payoneer almost everywhere. PayPal receiving is limited in a few reference countries (e.g. Bangladesh, Libya, Iraq); Payoneer/Wise or local alternatives may be needed.
