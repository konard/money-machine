## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$0 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $3,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **1/10**. Expect the first payment after 120–720 d.

## How it works

You upload complete or ongoing genre novels (romance, fantasy, werewolf) to Inkitt. Inkitt uses reader data to pick stories and offers authors contracts to publish them in Galatea, a pay-per-episode reading app, paying a revenue share or royalties. There is no open monetization; payment needs selection and a signed contract.

**Platforms:** Inkitt, Galatea
