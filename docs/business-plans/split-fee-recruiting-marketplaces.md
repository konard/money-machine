## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$0 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $15,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **2/10**. Expect the first payment after 45–240 d.

## How it works

Employers post roles with a fixed fee or % of salary. You source candidates with AI-assisted search and outreach, submit those who consent, and are paid after hire and guarantee. Paraform pays the listed per-role reward (its recruiter page shows an 80% success-fee split example); BountyJobs vets agencies by niche. Closing candidates stays human work.

**Platforms:** Paraform, BountyJobs (incl. CoSource split-fee)

## Legality & compliance

Legal; some countries license fee-charging placement (Japan: MHLW permission under the Employment Security Act; South Korea: registration under its Employment Security Act; Philippines: DOLE/DMW licensing). These generally bite when placing candidates in or from those countries; listed from legal knowledge, government pages not re-fetched (search budget exhausted). Candidate data is personal data; consent required before submission.

- **Restricted or banned in:** Japan (placement license), South Korea (placement registration), Philippines (overseas placement license)
- **Compliance requirements:** candidate consent before submission (Paraform terms); data-protection compliance for candidate data; local recruiting license where required; non-discrimination laws; tax reporting
- **Platform availability:** Roles mostly US. Paraform has active UK recruiters and its agreement covers international Stripe transfers, but no country list is published; BountyJobs states no country rule and vets agencies (niche or preferred-vendor) within 72h. ~50% payout availability is an estimate. Recruiter.com dropped: it now sells flat-fee on-demand recruiting, not a split-fee marketplace.
