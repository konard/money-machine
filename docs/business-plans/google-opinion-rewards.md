## How it works

Google's own survey app sends very short location- and purchase-based surveys a few times a week, paying about $0.10–$1 each. On Android the reward is Google Play credit (not withdrawable); on iOS rewards are paid to PayPal, so the bank path exists only through the iOS app. Income is tiny but near-certain where the app is available.

## Payout path

- **Methods:** PayPal (iOS), Google Play credit (Android)
- **Minimum payout:** Android: no threshold (Play credit); iOS PayPal threshold not documented officially
- **Frequency:** on request
- **Typical delay:** 2 days
- **Notes:** Official help ('Redeem rewards'): credit is applied to your Google payment profile, spent in the Play Store, and each credit expires one year after it is earned, so Android rewards never reach a bank. The survey landing page mentions 'Google Play or PayPal credit'; PayPal is the iOS reward, but the help centre no longer documents iOS availability or a cash-out minimum (historically $2).
