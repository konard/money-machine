## How it works

Once eligible (18+, 1,000+ followers in the US, 500+ in the UK, plus an account risk review), you add products from the TikTok Shop marketplace to your showcase and create demo/review videos or livestreams. Sellers set commissions (commonly 10-30%) and often send free samples. Sales happen inside TikTok, so conversion can be high, but it requires consistent on-camera content.
