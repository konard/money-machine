## How it works

Unlike commission marketplaces, Superprof charges students a 'Student Pass' to contact tutors and lets tutors list for free. Tutors set their price and either take payment directly (bank transfer, PayPal, Wise; 0% commission) or through Superprof (10% commission, or 0% on a paid Premium account). Apprentus works the same way in Europe, with a fee on the first lesson. You get a lead, run a trial, then keep the student long-term on your own terms.

## Legality & compliance

- **Compliance requirements:** self-employment / tax registration; safeguarding when tutoring minors; issue invoices or receipts
