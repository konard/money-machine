## How it works

You register or hand-pick brandable domains, then list them on Afternic, which inherited Dan.com's LTO system in 2024. Startups pay monthly installments; you keep ownership until fully paid, and if they default you keep the domain and payments. LTO max is $100,000 ($5,000 in California).

**Platforms:** Afternic (GoDaddy), Dan.com (legacy)

## Payout path

- **Methods:** PayPal, ACH (US), eCheck (non-US), wire transfer
- **Minimum payout:** PayPal $25 (no fee); ACH $10 ($0.90 fee); eCheck $25 ($5); wire $25 ($15-26)
- **Frequency:** monthly payouts as installments arrive
- **Typical delay:** 30 days
- **Notes:** LTO commission: 25% standard or 15% with GoDaddy Aftermarket nameservers ($15 minimum), +5% if Afternic Boost is on, minus a term discount (0% for up to 12 months, 5% for 13-24, 10% for 25-36, 15% for 37-60). Down payments pay full commission. Eligible Buy-It-Now prices $495 to $5M.
