## How it works

PHYSICAL: guests stay in your space, so cleaning, key handover and maintenance are unavoidable. Listing, pricing, messaging, reviews and payouts are online and largely automatable. Guests pay Airbnb at booking; Airbnb releases payout to you ~24 hours after check-in, minus host fee.

**Platforms:** Airbnb, Booking.com

## Payout path

- **Methods:** bank transfer, ACH (US), Fast Pay / pay to card (eligible regions), PayPal, Payoneer, international wire, Western Union
- **Minimum payout:** None
- **Frequency:** per booking
- **Typical delay:** 4 days
- **Notes:** Stays of 27 nights or less: payout initiated by end of the business day after scheduled check-in; hosts without two completed stays (unless listing verified) are paid after checkout; monthly stays first pay 28 days after check-in. Processing: Fast Pay/card 30 min, PayPal ~1 business day, bank 3-5 business days, wire 3-7. Reviews can delay payouts up to 45 days.
