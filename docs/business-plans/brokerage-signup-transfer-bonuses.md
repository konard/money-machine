## How it works

Brokers such as Robinhood, Webull, moomoo, Tiger Brokers, Trading 212 and Public give new customers free fractional shares, cash vouchers or 1-3% match on transferred assets. You open an account, deposit or transfer the qualifying amount, hold it for the required period (often 30-365 days), then sell the free shares and withdraw to your bank. The broker pays to acquire assets under management. Rewards are usually securities; some need capital at market risk.

**Platforms:** Robinhood, Webull, moomoo, Tiger Brokers, Trading 212, Public.com, Interactive Brokers referrals
