## How it works

Run a community server and sell through a Tebex store. Mojang's Usage Guidelines allow a single access price for everyone, cosmetics (not capes), non-competitive perks, donations with only server-wide rewards, and virtual currency that cannot be cashed out. Everything must be all-ages, with no gambling. Tebex is merchant of record (VAT, chargebacks) for a flat 5%.

**Platforms:** Tebex, Minecraft Java/Bedrock server software (Paper, Geyser), server lists

- **Notes:** Tebex charges a flat 5% (15% for FiveM) on its Starter (free) and Plus (GBP 12.49/mo) plans. ## Legality & compliance

Legal if Mojang's Usage Guidelines are followed: no loot boxes, gambling or pay-to-win, and in-game currency must not be cashable. Consumer-protection rules for minors apply (the EU and UK are strict).

- **Compliance requirements:** Minecraft Usage Guidelines (commercial/servers section); 'NOT AN OFFICIAL MINECRAFT' disclaimer; Privacy policy for player data; Tebex KYC
- **Platform availability:** Tebex pays out by PayPal, local bank transfer or wire; wire covers 200+ countries, and Tebex's wire list includes 46 of the 50 reference countries (missing Lebanon, Zimbabwe, Libya, Iraq). Needs a small monthly server rental (from about $10-20).
