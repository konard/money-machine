## How it works

PHYSICAL: you hand over the car (or use remote unlock) and clean it between trips. Everything else is online: listing, pricing, messaging, payment. Renters pay Turo; you receive a percentage of the trip price determined by the chosen protection plan. Works best in cities/airports with tourist demand.

**Platforms:** Turo, Getaround

## Payout path

- **Methods:** Stripe -> direct deposit/bank
- **Minimum payout:** No fixed minimum
- **Frequency:** per trip (Stripe, max one payout per day)
- **Typical delay:** 5 days
- **Notes:** US: Stripe initiates payout 3 business days after your first completed trip, then 3 hours after each later trip ends (partial payouts every 7 days for long trips), plus 3-5 business days bank processing. US hosts keep 70%, 80% or 90% of the trip price depending on the chosen earnings (protection) plan.

## Legality & compliance

Peer-to-peer car sharing is legal in most countries, but personal auto insurance may exclude commercial use; some jurisdictions require commercial registration.

- **Compliance requirements:** vehicle insurance compatibility; local tax/registration rules; Turo KYC
- **Platform availability:** Turo operates only in the US, Canada, UK, Australia and France (5 of 50 reference countries = 10%); Getaround coverage is similarly limited.
