## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$50 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $10,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **3/10**. Expect the first payment after 30–365 d.

## How it works

You publish a free newsletter to build a list, then gate premium issues (deep dives, data, research digests) behind a paid tier. Readers pay by card via Stripe; Substack keeps 10% plus Stripe fees, beehiiv and Ghost take 0% platform cut on paid subs (you pay the plan). AI can help aggregate sources, summarize and draft, but paying readers expect a distinct editorial voice and accuracy.

**Platforms:** Substack, beehiiv, Ghost

### Pricing strategy

Launch at $5-8/month with a founding-member tier ($100+/yr) for early supporters; raise prices for new subscribers once churn is under 5%/month and add an annual discount.
