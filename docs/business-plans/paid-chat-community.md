## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$150 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $10,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **3/10**. Expect the first payment after 14–180 d.

## How it works

Run a focused community (interview prep, niche hobby, language practice) where members pay monthly for access, resources and your presence. Whop/LaunchPass bill via Stripe and auto-grant Discord/Telegram roles; Skool bundles forum, courses and gamification. Trading/betting 'signals' groups are excluded.

**Platforms:** Whop, LaunchPass, Skool, Discord, Telegram

### Pricing strategy

## Legality & compliance

Legal; avoid unlicensed financial signals/gambling. Auto-renewal/cancellation laws apply.

- **Compliance requirements:** clear cancellation/refund terms; income/VAT reporting
- **Platform availability:** Whop pays out to 200+ countries (excluding sanctioned/restricted ones) via bank, PayPal or Coinbase; Skool/LaunchPass rely on Stripe and only pay out in Stripe countries (~35-40% of reference list).
