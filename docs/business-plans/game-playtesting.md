## How it works

Studios pay for recorded play sessions with think-aloud commentary. PlaytestCloud pays the fee stated in each invitation (commonly reported at about $9 for a 15-minute mobile test); BetaTesting and Antidote pay per project. You install the build, play as instructed, answer a survey, and are paid by PayPal after approval. Invitations depend on your device, country and demographics.

**Platforms:** PlaytestCloud, BetaTesting, Antidote.gg
