## How it works

Hotmart is a Brazil-born creator platform for courses and digital products, strongest in Latin America and Europe. You host the course in its member area, set the price and an affiliate commission, and list it in the affiliate marketplace. There, thousands of affiliates choose products to promote with their own traffic. That differs from Teachable or Udemy because sales can come from other people's marketing. Hotmart charges 9.9% plus a fixed USD/EUR 1.00 per sale (no monthly fee) and handles checkout, local payment methods and installments.
