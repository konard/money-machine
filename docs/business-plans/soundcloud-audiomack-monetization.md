## How it works

Unlike DSP distribution, these platforms let you upload directly and monetize on-platform plays. SoundCloud pays fan-powered royalties (based on each listener's actual listening time and the ad/subscription revenue they generate) on tracks monetized through a paid Artist or Artist Pro subscription; there is no minimum-stream requirement and approval takes about 5 business days. Audiomack's AMP is free for authenticated artists with 100+ followers and 50K plays in 6 months, or available immediately with an Audiomack Pro subscription; it is popular in Africa and the Caribbean. Only 100% original, fully owned music qualifies on both. You grow plays through reposts, playlists and community engagement.
