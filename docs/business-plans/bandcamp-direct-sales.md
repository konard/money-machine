## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$30 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $2,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **4/10**. Expect the first payment after 7–180 d.

## How it works

You upload releases with name-your-price or fixed pricing, add bonus digital items and optional fan subscriptions, and drive fans to the page from social media, mailing lists and Bandcamp's own discovery. Fans pay because they want to support artists directly and own downloads. Bandcamp Fridays (when Bandcamp waives its share) concentrate fan spending. Physical merch is possible but involves shipping.

## Legality & compliance

Selling your own music is legal everywhere; taxable. Covers require mechanical licenses for sales in some jurisdictions.

- **Compliance requirements:** own the masters/compositions or license covers; sales tax/VAT handled per Bandcamp policy; tax reporting
- **Platform availability:** Bandcamp artist accounts are open internationally, but payouts go only to PayPal, which cannot receive funds in a few reference countries (e.g. Bangladesh, Libya, Iraq). Physical merch adds shipping.

## Risks

- Needs an audience; discovery is limited
- Ownership changes (Songtradr acquired Bandcamp in 2023) could alter terms
- Bandcamp Fridays are confirmed only through Dec 2026
