## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$2–$20 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $1,500 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **9/10**. Expect the first payment after 30–120 d.

## How it works

Robo-advisors (Betterment, Wealthfront, Nutmeg, Scalable Capital, StashAway, Syfe, Wahed, Bamboo/Risevest, Groww) automatically buy and rebalance low-cost index ETFs for a 0.25-0.75% fee. Companies in the index pay dividends (~1.5-4% yield), which you can withdraw or reinvest. Income is passive but scales with capital and carries market risk; it is not a quick money maker.

**Platforms:** Betterment, Wealthfront, Nutmeg, Scalable Capital, StashAway, Syfe, Wahed, local index funds

## Payout path

- **Methods:** dividend cash -> withdraw to bank, sell units -> bank
- **Minimum payout:** Usually none; some platforms $1-$100 minimum withdrawal
- **Frequency:** dividends quarterly; withdrawals 2-5 days
- **Typical delay:** 90 days
- **Notes:** First dividend arrives at next quarterly distribution. Betterment (Nov 2026): 0.25%/year with $200+/month deposits or $24k+ balance, otherwise $5/month, which eats small balances.

## Legality & compliance

Investing in domestic funds is legal everywhere (hence 100%). The listed countries restrict only the foreign robo-advisor route under capital controls - use domestic or Sharia-compliant funds there.

- **Restricted or banned in:** Bangladesh, Nepal, Tunisia, Morocco, Libya, Iraq
- **Compliance requirements:** KYC and suitability questionnaire; dividend and capital-gains tax; W-8BEN/withholding treaties
- **Platform availability:** Local robo-advisors or index funds exist in most reference countries; the global platforms cover US/UK/EU/SG/MY/AU. Wahed operates in several Muslim-majority countries.
