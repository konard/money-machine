## How it works

You compose/produce royalty-free-friendly background tracks (corporate, cinematic, lo-fi) and upload them non-exclusively to Pond5 and Envato AudioJungle, where video creators buy licenses. Pond5 pays a 30% royalty share on music for non-exclusive artists. Curated libraries (Musicbed, Artlist Original) are invite/application-only but pay larger sync fees or buyouts. Income compounds with catalog size.

## What can be automated

- Script metadata/keyword/description generation per track with an LLM
- Batch-render stems, loops and 15/30/60s cutdowns from each master
- Bulk upload via CSV/FTP where supported

## Unavoidable manual steps

- Composing/producing the music
- Contributor KYC and tax forms
- Curated library applications

## Legality & compliance

Licensing your own copyrighted works is legal everywhere; income is taxable. Fully AI-generated audio is restricted or banned by many libraries.

- **Compliance requirements:** own 100% of composition and master (no uncleared samples); platform tax form (W-8BEN for non-US); disclose AI use per library policy
- **Platform availability:** Pond5/Envato accept contributors in most countries; payout via PayPal/Payoneer limits a few (e.g. PayPal receive limits in Bangladesh, Nepal, Libya, Iraq).

## Risks

- Oversupplied market; most tracks never sell
- Royalty cuts and policy changes
- Library closures (Songtradr Marketplace 2026)
- Do not register non-exclusive tracks in Content ID (causes false claims on buyers)
