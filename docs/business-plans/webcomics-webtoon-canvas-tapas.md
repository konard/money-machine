## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$30 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $2,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **2/10**. Expect the first payment after 90–540 d.

## How it works

You publish episodes on WEBTOON CANVAS and/or Tapas. WEBTOON pays 50% of ad revenue once a series has 1,000 subscribers and 40,000 global monthly page views, and fans can send paid Super Likes once a series has 500 likes. Tapas shares about 70% of ad revenue from 100 subscribers and passes on 100% of Ink tips (after third-party fees) from 250 subscribers; creators must be 18+. Writers without drawing skill can partner with an artist; the platforms' AI-art rules should be checked first.

**Platforms:** WEBTOON CANVAS, Tapas
