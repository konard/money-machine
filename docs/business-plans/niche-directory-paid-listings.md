## Realistic expectations

## How it works

Seed a directory with accurate, verified entries from public data, add useful filters and editorial 'best X' pages, and grow organic traffic. Revenue comes from vendors paying for featured placement, verified badges, sponsored slots, plus affiliate links on listings. Paid placements must be labelled as sponsored and must not masquerade as editorial rankings. Payers are businesses seeking visibility.

**Platforms:** Stripe, directory software (WordPress/Directorist, static site), affiliate networks

### Pricing strategy

Offer free basic listings and a cheap launch-price featured slot ($10-20) to the first 10 vendors, then raise prices as traffic grows and add annual plans.
