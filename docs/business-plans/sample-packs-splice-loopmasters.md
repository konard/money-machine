## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$50 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $3,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **3/10**. Expect the first payment after 60–365 d.

## How it works

You build themed packs (drums, vocal chops, Serum/Vital presets, MIDI progressions) and either apply as a provider/label on Splice, where subscribers spend credits per sound and creators are paid per download, or register as a vendor/label on stores such as Producer Loops (50% royalty) or pitch Loopmasters/ADSR, which sell whole packs and pay a royalty share. You can also sell packs directly on Gumroad/your own store. Producers pay because ready-made, cleared sounds save them time.

**Platforms:** Splice Sounds, Loopmasters, Producer Loops, ADSR Sounds, Gumroad

## Legality & compliance

Legal everywhere; you license your own recordings royalty-free. Must not contain uncleared third-party audio.

- **Compliance requirements:** 100% original or cleared content; provider/label agreement; tax forms; disclose AI generation where label requires
- **Platform availability:** Producer Loops vendor registration is self-serve and pays by PayPal (bank transfer for UK providers); Splice and Loopmasters accept creators internationally, but only after curation. PayPal receiving is limited in a few reference countries (e.g. Bangladesh, Libya, Iraq); Payoneer/Wise or local alternatives may be needed.

## Risks

- Curated access: many applicants rejected
- Low per-download rates
- Label consolidation and closures
- Piracy of direct-sold packs
- Producer Loops now pays quarterly with a £100 minimum, so small sellers may wait several quarters

## Evidence that people get paid

Splice reports paying sound creators over $25M since launching Splice Sounds; Producer Loops' provider terms publish a 50% royalty with quarterly PayPal/bank payouts.
