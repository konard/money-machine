## How it works

Create a Ko-fi or Buy Me a Coffee page and link it from READMEs, videos, blogs and bios. Readers tip $3-$10 or subscribe monthly. Ko-fi takes 0% on donations (5% on memberships/shop unless Gold) and pays into your PayPal/Stripe; Buy Me a Coffee takes 5% (no monthly fee) and pays out to your bank via Stripe/Wise in 44 countries.

**Platforms:** Ko-fi, Buy Me a Coffee
