## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$10 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $5,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **4/10**. Expect the first payment after 100–365 d.

## How it works

Upload original mods for PC games and opt each mod page into Mod Rewards. Nexus pays a monthly pool (GBP 325,000 in March 2024) to authors using an undisclosed algorithm based on user interactions, with repeat interactions from the same user counting for less. Each Donation Point is worth $0.001 (1,000 DP = $1) and can be redeemed for cash via PayPal, games or Premium.

**Platforms:** Nexus Mods

## Payout path

- **Methods:** PayPal cash redemption (Nexus commits to always offering it)
- **Minimum payout:** Not stated in official help articles; redemptions only in the amounts listed in the DP store
- **Frequency:** monthly
- **Typical delay:** 105 days
- **Notes:** NET 90: DP for a month is credited about three months later (January's DP arrives at the start of May). Nexus can change the cash value of a DP at any time. DP currently do not expire. Where a cash reward is blocked by national or provider restrictions, Nexus refunds the DP and offers no alternative.

## Legality & compliance

Legal. Files must be your original work or assets cleared for commercial use. Splitting one mod into several pages to farm DP is disqualified.

- **Compliance requirements:** 18+; opt-in per mod; asset permissions; tax reporting
- **Platform availability:** Cash redemption depends on PayPal, which can receive payments in about 45 of 50 reference countries; elsewhere DP are refunded (spendable only on games/Premium).
