## How it works

You design printable PDFs/PNGs or SVG cut files and list them as Etsy digital items; Etsy delivers the file automatically. Buyers come from Etsy search. Fees: $0.20 listing, 6.5% transaction, ~3% + $0.25 payment processing (US) (+ Offsite Ads 12-15%). AI-assisted designs are allowed if made from your own prompts/designs and disclosed per Etsy Creativity Standards.

## Payout path

- **Methods:** Etsy Payments -> bank, Etsy Payments via Payoneer (required in e.g. India, Japan, Ukraine, Brazil, Argentina, Thailand, Peru, Serbia, Georgia, South Korea)
- **Minimum payout:** No minimum on the default weekly schedule; daily deposits need a local minimum (e.g. USD 25, GBP 15, EUR 20)
- **Frequency:** weekly (Monday) by default; daily, biweekly or monthly selectable
- **Typical delay:** 14 days
- **Notes:** New sellers' funds are generally eligible for deposit 14 days after a sale; Etsy may apply a payment account reserve to new shops; 5-day hold after changing bank details.
