## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$50 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $3,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **3/10**. Expect the first payment after 30–365 d.

## How it works

If you run docs, a dev blog or a newsletter with ~50k monthly pageviews, EthicalAds places contextual developer ads and pays 70% of revenue (about $2.50 CPM for US/EU traffic). Alternatively list ad slots on BuySellAds or sell fixed-price sponsor slots directly with a Stripe payment link. Advertisers pay to reach developers without tracking.

**Platforms:** EthicalAds, BuySellAds, Stripe
