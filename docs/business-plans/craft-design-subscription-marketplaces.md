## How it works

Cricut/Silhouette/laser and embroidery hobbyists buy files individually or via all-access subscriptions. Designers are paid per individual sale plus a share of the subscription pool weighted by downloads, so a large catalogue earns small recurring amounts. Unlike Etsy you pay no listing fees and the marketplace brings its own traffic. Bundle deals and freebies feature designers to new buyers.

**Platforms:** Creative Fabrica, Design Bundles, So Fontsy
