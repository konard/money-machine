## How it works

Salad resells distributed consumer GPU capacity to AI companies (image generation, transcription, batch inference). You install the app on a Windows gaming PC; when idle, it runs workloads and credits your Salad balance. You redeem from the storefront, choosing PayPal where supported (gift cards otherwise). Unlike Vast you do not set prices; it is consumer-grade and simpler, but pays less.
