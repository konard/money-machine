Most people who try this earn around **$3–$20 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $150 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **8/10**. Expect the first payment after 3–30 d.

## How it works

Market-research and NGO clients pay for ground-truth data. Apps publish tasks near you (take a photo of a product display, confirm a store is open, report local fuel prices, answer a short survey) paying roughly $0.25–$10 each. Premise pays contributors via PayPal, Payoneer or Bitcoin depending on country; Roamler covers Europe and the US. Tasks require leaving the house, but payment is reliable and these apps are among the few that pay residents of lower-income reference countries directly.

- **Platform availability:** Premise recruits in many lower-income countries (country list not published on the contributor page; reported 100+ including Kenya, Nigeria, Ghana, Philippines, Indonesia, India, Colombia, Mexico, Brazil). Roamler: Europe and a US programme.
