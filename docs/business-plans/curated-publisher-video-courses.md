Most people who try this earn around **$0–$0 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $3,000 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **2/10**. Expect the first payment after 90–365 d.

## How it works

Curated course publishers recruit experts by application. LinkedIn Learning wants a teaching video sample and proposal. Pluralsight asks for an audition video, and its video-course pay is partly based on viewership, paid quarterly. Domestika covers production for creative courses and pays via PayPal. Once accepted, the publisher handles editing, marketing and enterprise distribution. That's different from self-publishing on Udemy or Skillshare.
