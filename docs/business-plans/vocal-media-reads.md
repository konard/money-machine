## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$0–$5 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $300 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **4/10**. Expect the first payment after 30–180 d.

## How it works

You publish original stories, essays and poems on Vocal's community sites and share them. Vocal pays a fixed rate per 1,000 reads from its own revenue, readers can tip, and paying Vocal+ members can enter writing challenges with cash prizes. Earnings per story are tiny; the realistic path is challenge wins. Because challenge entry needs a paid membership, treat the free tier as the zero-capital version.

**Platforms:** Vocal (vocal.media)
