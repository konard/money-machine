## How it works

Creators and businesses buy narration for explainers and ads, and podcasters pay for edited, leveled episodes. AI voices (licensed for commercial use) and tools like Descript/Auphonic do most of the work; you direct, fix pronunciation and QA. Separately, a clone of YOUR OWN voice shared in the ElevenLabs Voice Library earns royalties on paid usage.

**Platforms:** Fiverr, Upwork, ElevenLabs Voice Library
