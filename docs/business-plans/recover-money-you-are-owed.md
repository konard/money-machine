## How it works

Governments hold dormant bank balances, uncashed cheques, deposits and insurance proceeds in unclaimed-property registries that anyone can search free (US: each state's registry and MissingMoney.com; UK: My Lost Account, Gretel; Canada: Bank of Canada; Australia: ASIC MoneySmart). Class-action settlements publish claim forms where eligible consumers get cash. Many people are also owed tax refunds, energy or telecom overpayments, or forgotten pension pots. This is not income, but it is money in a bank account with no risk and a short online form, and it is one of the highest-probability 'payouts' for anyone who has never looked.

**Platforms:** MissingMoney.com (US), state unclaimed-property sites, ClassAction.org / TopClassActions settlement lists, My Lost Account (UK), Bank of Canada unclaimed balances, ASIC MoneySmart (AU)

## Legality & compliance

Claiming your own property is legal everywhere. Filing settlement claims you are not eligible for is fraud; only claim where you genuinely qualify.

- **Compliance requirements:** identity proof; only claim what you are eligible for
- **Platform availability:** Formal unclaimed-property registries exist in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and several EU and Asian countries; class-action claim systems are mainly US.
