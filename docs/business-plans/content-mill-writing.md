## Realistic expectations

Most people who try this earn around **$20–$150 per month** in the first year; the high figure of $1,200 is a ceiling for a focused solo operator, not a typical outcome. The chance of receiving at least one real payout within six months is rated **7/10**. Expect the first payment after 7–30 d.

## How it works

Content platforms hold a queue of client orders that any approved writer can claim. Textbroker US pays per word by quality rating (3 stars 1.1¢, 4 stars 1.6¢, 5 stars 5.5¢; DirectOrders from 2.2¢) with payouts processed twice a week; Verblio pays per accepted piece and lets writers invoice weekly. Textbroker rejects AI-generated writing and runs every submission through AI detection, so AI can help research and outline but the prose must be your own. Income is modest, but orders are usually available and payouts are dependable.

## Legality & compliance

Legal everywhere. Platform rules: original work, no plagiarism; Textbroker states 'AI-generated writing is not accepted' and checks every submission. Platform availability is limited by residency rules, not legality.

- **Compliance requirements:** original writing; no AI-generated text where banned; tax reporting
- **Platform availability:** Textbroker US requires permanent US residence and US ID; Verblio requires US citizenship and an SSN. Textbroker runs separate national sites (e.g. Germany, France, Spain, Italy, Netherlands) whose residency rules were not re-checked. Writers outside these markets have few options here.
