## How it works

You construct themed crosswords with free or paid construction software and word lists, then submit to outlets like The New York Times, Universal and the LA Times that accept freelance puzzles. Editors buy puzzles that meet strict specs. Pay is a flat fee per puzzle. Software automates grid filling; theme ideas and clueing are the human part.

**Platforms:** The New York Times Crossword, Universal Crossword, LA Times Crossword
