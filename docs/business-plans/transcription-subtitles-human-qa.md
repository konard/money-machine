## How it works

YouTubers, podcasters and course creators pay for accurate transcripts and timed captions. ASR gives a 90-95% draft; you fix names, jargon and timing, then deliver SRT/VTT/DOCX. Translated subtitles are an upsell.

**Platforms:** Fiverr, Upwork, direct (Stripe/PayPal invoices)

Automatic captions on YouTube, Descript, CapCut and Zoom are now good enough for most creators, so plain transcription demand and prices have fallen sharply since 2023. Remaining paid demand is accuracy-critical work: legal/medical/research interviews, multi-speaker audio, translated and burned-in subtitles. The median starter earns little; specialists in a language pair or niche do better.

### Pricing strategy

Price per audio minute with surcharges for poor audio, multiple speakers and 24h turnaround; start low for reviews, then lead with translated/burned-in subtitle packages.

## Legality & compliance

Legal everywhere; taxable. Legal/medical transcription can have confidentiality rules (e.g. HIPAA in the US); avoid unless compliant.

- **Compliance requirements:** tax reporting; platform ID check; Fiverr AI standards / Upwork no-misrepresentation; confidentiality of client audio
- **Platform availability:** Fiverr/Upwork pay out almost everywhere via Payoneer; PayPal receiving missing in some (e.g. Bangladesh).
