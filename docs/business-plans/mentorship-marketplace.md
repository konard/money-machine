## How it works

You apply as a mentor (typically software, design, data, product or career experience) and publish monthly plans, e.g. $150/month for async chat plus 2 calls. Mentees discover you via MentorCruise search and pay a subscription; MentorCruise adds a ~20% markup on top (min $20, capped at $100 for $100-$800 plans; one-off sessions carry a $9-$39 flat fee). Example: $100 plan -> mentee pays $120, mentor gets $100 minus card fees (~$96.40). Payouts via Stripe Connect or bank transfer.
