## How it works

Workana connects mostly Latin American SMB clients with freelancers in writing, design, IT, data entry and marketing. You build a profile, send proposals and deliver via milestones. Clients prefer Spanish/Portuguese speakers, so competition with English-only global freelancers is lower. Commission falls as a client relationship grows.

### Pricing strategy

Underprice the first 3-5 small projects to earn reviews, then raise rates 20-30% and steer repeat clients toward larger milestones where the fee falls to 10%.

### Path to profit

1. Month 0: profile, portfolio of 3 samples, 20 proposals (cost $0)
2. Month 1-2: first 1-3 small projects; expect $50-300
3. Month 3-6: reviews unlock better projects; expect $150-500/month
4. Month 6-12: repeat clients at lower fees; median ~$250/month

## Payout path

- **Methods:** Payoneer -> local bank, PayPal -> bank, Payoneer -> Airtm (13 LatAm countries)
- **Minimum payout:** USD 50 for Payoneer bank transfer; PayPal per-country
- **Frequency:** twice monthly, or weekly on paid Explorer/Master plans
- **Typical delay:** 5 days
- **Notes:** Freelancer commission 20% up to USD 300 per client, 10% to USD 3,000, 5% above. Methods depend on country. Airtm and local Payoneer partners (Prex, Ligo, Santander AR) give local-currency cash-out.

## Legality & compliance

Freelancing is legal everywhere in the reference set; income is taxable. Off-platform payment is banned without the voluntary-exclusion fee.

- **Compliance requirements:** tax reporting; Payoneer/PayPal KYC; no disintermediation
- **Platform availability:** Open worldwide; demand is Spanish/Portuguese. PayPal receiving unavailable in Nigeria, Lebanon, Bangladesh, Libya, Iraq; Payoneer covers nearly all.
