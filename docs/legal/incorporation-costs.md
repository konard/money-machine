## Country details

### AO Angola

**Sole proprietor.** Registration: before earning (Register as an 'empresário em nome individual' with a NIF before trading; simplified tax regime up to AOA 25M turnover). Cost $40, annual $100, about 7 days, online: no. Registration goes through the Guiché Único da Empresa (GUE) and the AGT for a NIF. Self-employed income falls under IRT Group B. Most steps are in person. Register: <https://www.gue.gov.ao>

**Company.** Sociedade por Quotas (Lda) / Sociedade Unipessoal por Quotas: government fee $200, typical all-in $1,500, minimum capital $0, annual compliance about $2,500, about 15 days, online: no, 100% foreign ownership: yes, local director required: no. Formed through GUE. Share capital is set freely (the old Kz 100,000 minimum no longer applies). The Private Investment Law 2018 allows 100% foreign ownership in most sectors. Accounting and tax compliance usually need a certified accountant. Register: <https://www.gue.gov.ao>

**Tax.** Corporate 25%, personal top rate 25%, VAT 14% above $26,550. Small-business regime: Simplified regime for taxpayers with turnover of AOA 25M or less (no VAT charged). Exports of services are VAT-exempt. Paying foreign service providers requires BNA licensing and withholding.

**Foreign currency.** Capital controls: approval needed. BNA licenses foreign exchange. PayPal does not support receiving in Angola, and Payoneer or Wise withdrawals to local banks can be difficult. Bringing in and converting foreign income involves banking documentation.

Sources: <https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/angola/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income>, <https://www.gue.gov.ao>, <https://portaldocontribuinte.minfin.gov.ao>, <https://open.er-api.com/v6/latest/USD>

_Confidence: estimated; last checked 2026-11-03._

### AR Argentina

**Sole proprietor.** Registration: before earning (Must register with ARCA (ex-AFIP) as monotributista or autónomo before invoicing; no threshold.). Cost $0, annual $385, about 1 days, online: yes. 'Monotributo' simplified regime: a single monthly payment (from 1 Aug 2026: category A ARS 49,527.18/month for services, up to ARS 12.0M gross income a year; top category K ARS 1,614,446/month, up to ARS 126.6M; indexed twice yearly) covers income tax, VAT and social security. Otherwise 'responsable inscripto'. Register: <https://www.afip.gob.ar/monotributo/>

**Company.** Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada (SAS) or SRL: government fee $150, typical all-in $900, minimum capital $450, annual compliance about $2,500, about 20 days, online: yes, 100% foreign ownership: yes, local director required: no. SAS minimum capital is 2 minimum monthly wages (~USD 450). IGJ (Buenos Aires) procedures and accountant/notary fees vary widely. Costs in USD swing with the exchange rate. Register: <https://www.argentina.gob.ar/justicia/igj>

**Tax.** Corporate 35%, personal top rate 35%, VAT 21% above $0. Small-business regime: Monotributo: fixed monthly fee by category (A-K) bundling tax and social security. Progressive CIT 25-35%. Exports of services are VAT-exempt (VAT credits refundable). Monotributistas can invoice foreign clients with Factura E.

**Foreign currency.** Capital controls: reporting only. The FX 'cepo' was largely lifted for individuals in April 2025. Companies may still face rules to liquidate service-export proceeds in the official market. Payoneer and Wise are commonly used; PayPal withdrawals are limited.

Sources: <https://www.afip.gob.ar/monotributo/>, <https://www.argentina.gob.ar/justicia/igj>, <https://www.arca.gob.ar/monotributo/categorias.asp>, <https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/quick-charts/corporate-income-tax-cit-rates>, <https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/quick-charts/personal-income-tax-pit-rates>, <https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/quick-charts/value-added-tax-vat-rates>

### AM Armenia

**Sole proprietor.** Registration: before earning (Individual entrepreneurs must register before carrying out business; no threshold.). Cost $0, annual $300, about 1 days, online: yes. 'Individual entrepreneur' (անհատ ձեռնարկատեր, IE). State registration is free and online via e-register. Can opt into turnover tax (10% for most activities, including services; trade 10% less 9.5% of documented purchases) up to AMD 115M, or microbusiness status (tax-exempt for eligible activities up to AMD 24M). Register: <https://e-register.moj.am/en/>

**Company.** Limited Liability Company (LLC, ՍՊԸ): government fee $0, typical all-in $300, minimum capital $0, annual compliance about $1,200, about 2 days, online: yes, 100% foreign ownership: yes, local director required: no. Online state registration is free (a AMD 600,000 fee applies only to names containing "Hay", "Armenia" or "Armenian"). No minimum capital. Registration with the template charter is immediate; otherwise up to 2 business days. Register: <https://e-register.moj.am/en/>

**Tax.** Corporate 18%, personal top rate 20%, VAT 20% above $319,004. Small-business regime: Turnover tax up to AMD 115M (10% for most activities incl. services; 1% for listed high-tech activities); microbusiness regime (exempt, limited activity list) up to AMD 24M. Services whose place of supply is outside Armenia (e.g. to foreign clients) are not subject to Armenian VAT. The IT-startup certificate regime has ended for new applicants.

**Foreign currency.** Capital controls: none. No controls; AMD is freely convertible. Wise and Payoneer work. PayPal sends only, with limited receiving.

Sources: <https://e-register.moj.am/en/>, <https://www.petekamutner.am>, <https://e-register.moj.am/en/services/d0fd5a23-a8bf-4103-bedd-ef6a6db9cdda>, <https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/armenia/corporate/other-taxes>, <https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/quick-charts/corporate-income-tax-cit-rates>, <https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/quick-charts/personal-income-tax-pit-rates>, <https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/quick-charts/value-added-tax-vat-rates>

### AU Australia

**Sole proprietor.** Registration: before earning (An ABN is needed to invoice businesses (free). GST registration is mandatory at AUD 75,000 annual turnover. Business-name registration is needed only if not trading under your own name.). Cost $0, annual $0, about 1 days, online: yes. Sole trader. Registering a business name costs about AUD 45 for 1 year or AUD 104 for 3 years with ASIC (indexed). Register: <https://www.abr.gov.au>

**Company.** Proprietary company (Pty Ltd): government fee $403, typical all-in $700, minimum capital $0, annual compliance about $2,200, about 1 days, online: yes, 100% foreign ownership: yes, local director required: yes. The ASIC registration fee is about AUD 611 and the annual review fee about AUD 329 (both indexed each 1 July; figures estimated). At least one director must ordinarily reside in Australia, which a resident founder satisfies. A Director ID is required (free). Register: <https://asic.gov.au/for-business/registering-a-company/>

**Tax.** Corporate 25%, personal top rate 47%, VAT 10% above $49,500. Small-business regime: Base-rate entity CIT 25% (aggregated turnover < AUD 50M); small business income tax offset for sole traders. Services supplied to non-residents outside Australia are generally GST-free (exports). The top rate is 45% plus the 2% Medicare levy.

**Foreign currency.** Capital controls: none. No controls; AUSTRAC reporting applies to international transfers. PayPal, Wise and Payoneer work.

Sources: <https://asic.gov.au/for-business/registering-a-company/>, <https://www.abr.gov.au>, <https://www.ato.gov.au>, <https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/australia/corporate/other-taxes>, <https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/quick-charts/corporate-income-tax-cit-rates>, <https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/quick-charts/personal-income-tax-pit-rates>, <https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/quick-charts/value-added-tax-vat-rates>

### BD Bangladesh

**Sole proprietor.** Registration: before earning (Get an e-TIN and a trade licence from the city corporation or municipality. Turnover tax (VAT enlistment) applies from BDT 5M, and full VAT from BDT 30M). Cost $25, annual $25, about 7 days, online: yes. A sole proprietorship needs a trade licence and an e-TIN. IT and ITES freelancers can get an ICT Division freelancer ID, and IT and ITES income is tax-exempt until 30 June 2027 subject to conditions such as cashless receipts. Register: <https://etaxnbr.gov.bd>

**Company.** Private Limited Company / One Person Company (OPC): government fee $200, typical all-in $600, minimum capital $0, annual compliance about $900, about 10 days, online: yes, 100% foreign ownership: yes, local director required: no. Registered online with the RJSC; fees scale with authorised capital. An OPC needs BDT 2.5M to 50M paid-up capital. Audits are mandatory, and foreign-owned companies register with BIDA. Register: <https://roc.gov.bd>

**Tax.** Corporate 27.5%, personal top rate 30%, VAT 15% above $241,000. Small-business regime: VAT turnover tax at 4% for turnover of BDT 5M to 30M; a 1% minimum tax on gross receipts; the IT and ITES income exemption until June 2027. The corporate rate shown is for non-listed companies (25% if all transactions are cashless). Export of IT services is VAT zero-rated, and software and ITES exports get Bangladesh Bank cash incentives.

**Foreign currency.** Capital controls: approval needed. Bangladesh Bank exchange controls apply, and export proceeds must be repatriated. PayPal receiving is not available (Xoom inbound only). Payoneer and Wise into local bank accounts are the norm, and freelancers can open ERQ foreign-currency accounts.

Sources: <https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/bangladesh/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income>, <https://roc.gov.bd>, <https://nbr.gov.bd>, <https://open.er-api.com/v6/latest/USD>

_Confidence: estimated; last checked 2026-11-03._

### BR Brazil

**Sole proprietor.** Registration: before earning (MEI registration (free, online via gov.br) is needed to issue invoices. MEI caps annual revenue at BRL 81,000; above that you must migrate to an ME under Simples Nacional.). Cost $0, annual $185, about 1 days, online: yes. 'MEI' (Microempreendedor Individual): monthly DAS of about BRL 80-86 (5% of minimum wage for INSS plus ISS/ICMS). Many intellectual professions (developers, consultants) are not eligible and must open an ME/SLU. Register: <https://www.gov.br/empresas-e-negocios/pt-br/empreendedor>

**Company.** Sociedade Limitada Unipessoal (SLU) / LTDA: government fee $150, typical all-in $500, minimum capital $0, annual compliance about $1,500, about 10 days, online: yes, 100% foreign ownership: yes, local director required: no. Junta Comercial plus municipal fees are about BRL 500-1,000. A contador is effectively mandatory (~BRL 400-800/month). No minimum capital. The administrator must be resident in Brazil. Register: <https://www.gov.br/empresas-e-negocios/pt-br/redesim>

**Tax.** Corporate 34%, personal top rate 27.5%, VAT 0% (no registration threshold). Small-business regime: Simples Nacional up to BRL 4.8M (services from ~6% of revenue, Annex III/V with Fator R); MEI fixed monthly fee up to BRL 81,000. No single VAT yet. ISS (2-5%) plus PIS/COFINS apply, but exported services are exempt from ISS and PIS/COFINS. CBS/IBS dual VAT is being phased in from 2026 (test rates) through 2033. The 34% CIT is IRPJ 15% + 10% surtax + CSLL 9%.

**Foreign currency.** Capital controls: reporting only. FX goes through authorised institutions with IOF tax. Inbound transfers are freely received; Wise, Payoneer and PayPal withdrawals work.

Sources: <https://www.gov.br/empresas-e-negocios/pt-br/empreendedor>, <https://www.gov.br/receitafederal>, <https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/brazil/corporate/other-taxes>, <https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/quick-charts/corporate-income-tax-cit-rates>, <https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/quick-charts/personal-income-tax-pit-rates>, <https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/quick-charts/value-added-tax-vat-rates>

### KH Cambodia

**Sole proprietor.** Registration: above income threshold (Sole proprietors with turnover above KHR 250M (about USD 61k) a year must register as small taxpayers with the GDT and the Ministry of Commerce). Cost $150, annual $100, about 14 days, online: yes. A sole proprietorship registers through the Ministry of Commerce's online business registration (single portal) and the GDT. It pays a patent tax each year and progressive tax of 0–20% on profit. Register: <https://www.businessregistration.moc.gov.kh>

**Company.** Private Limited Company (Co., Ltd.): government fee $300, typical all-in $1,000, minimum capital $974, annual compliance about $1,800, about 14 days, online: yes, 100% foreign ownership: yes, local director required: no. Registered online through the MoC single portal. Minimum capital is KHR 4M (about USD 1,000). Companies are medium taxpayers by default, with monthly filings and an annual patent tax. Foreigners can own 100% except land. Register: <https://www.businessregistration.moc.gov.kh>

**Tax.** Corporate 20%, personal top rate 20%, VAT 10% above $60,900. Small-business regime: Small taxpayers pay progressive rates of 0–20%; a 1% minimum tax applies if proper accounting records are not kept. The economy is dollarised and the VAT threshold is KHR 250M. Non-resident e-commerce suppliers charge 10% VAT, and exported services are zero-rated.

**Foreign currency.** Capital controls: none. No capital controls. USD is widely used. PayPal receiving is limited, but Payoneer, Wise and local banks such as ABA work.

Sources: <https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cambodia/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income>, <https://www.businessregistration.moc.gov.kh>, <https://www.tax.gov.kh>, <https://open.er-api.com/v6/latest/USD>

### CA Canada

**Sole proprietor.** Registration: not required (No registration needed when trading under your own legal name in most provinces; GST/HST registration is mandatory once worldwide taxable supplies exceed CAD 30,000 in four consecutive quarters.). Cost $0, annual $0, about 1 days, online: yes. Sole proprietor; business income is reported on form T2125 with the T1 return. Registering a trade name costs about CAD 60 in Ontario (varies by province). Register: <https://www.canada.ca/en/services/business/start.html>

**Company.** Corporation (federal CBCA or provincial, e.g. Ontario): government fee $143, typical all-in $400, minimum capital $0, annual compliance about $1,500, about 2 days, online: yes, 100% foreign ownership: yes, local director required: yes. Federal online incorporation costs CAD 200 (1 business day; CAD 100 extra for 4-hour express) and a NUANS report is no longer needed for an online word name; Ontario charges CAD 300. The federal annual return is CAD 12. The CBCA requires 25% resident-Canadian directors, which a resident founder satisfies; several provinces dropped this rule. Register: <https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/corporations-canada/en>

**Tax.** Corporate 12%, personal top rate 54%, VAT 5% above $21,700. Small-business regime: Small business deduction: ~9% federal + provincial (combined ~11-12.2%) on the first CAD 500,000 of active business income. GST 5% or HST 13-15% by province. Services supplied to non-residents are generally zero-rated. Digital platforms collect GST/HST on B2C supplies.

**Foreign currency.** Capital controls: none. No controls. Large incoming cross-border transfers (CAD 10,000+) are reported by the institution to FINTRAC. PayPal, Wise and Payoneer all work.

Sources: <https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/corporations-canada/en>, <https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency.html>, <https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/corporations-canada/en/services-fees-and-processing-times>, <https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/quick-charts/corporate-income-tax-cit-rates>, <https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/quick-charts/personal-income-tax-pit-rates>, <https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/quick-charts/value-added-tax-vat-rates>

### CL Chile

**Sole proprietor.** Registration: before earning (Must file 'inicio de actividades' with SII before invoicing; no income threshold.). Cost $0, annual $600, about 1 days, online: yes. Self-employed professional ('persona natural con boletas de honorarios') or 'empresario individual' (EIRL optional). Honorarios carry a provisional withholding/retention of about 15.25% in 2026, rising yearly to 17% by 2028. Register: <https://www.sii.cl>

**Company.** Sociedad por Acciones (SpA) via 'Tu Empresa en un Día': government fee $20, typical all-in $300, minimum capital $0, annual compliance about $1,300, about 2 days, online: yes, 100% foreign ownership: yes, local director required: no. Formation in the online Registro de Empresas y Sociedades is free apart from an advanced electronic signature (~USD 20). No minimum capital. Needs a monthly accountant for F29 VAT returns. Register: <https://www.registrodeempresasysociedades.cl>

**Tax.** Corporate 12.5%, personal top rate 40%, VAT 19% above $0. Small-business regime: Pro-PYME regime (Art. 14 D) temporarily taxed at 12.5% for 2025-2027 (15% in 2028, 23% from 2029); general regime 27% in 2026, falling to 23% from 2029; honorarios for individuals carry no VAT.
